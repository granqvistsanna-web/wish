# Synopsis

## Premise
Amma Lindqvist — a woman born on the wrong side of the Wall, raised as a Capitol agent — arrives at Station 7-Syd under a false name to investigate a production anomaly in module 7-12. The station's previous technician, Maja, died in what was ruled an electrical accident. Amma is supposed to confirm the accident, file her report, and leave. Instead, she finds a small community bound by secrets, a station commander named Cassia who sees too much, and a mystery that forces her to choose between her mission and the people she was sent to betray.

## The Hook
Amma arrives at Station 7-Syd in killing heat, carrying false papers and a bad shoulder. She is met not by the commander but by Karin, who takes one look at her and knows she's from the wrong side of the Wall. Within her first evening, Amma realizes this assignment is nothing like the briefing described — and that Cassia, the station commander, is nothing like anyone she's ever met.

## Act One — Setup (Chapters 1–5)
Amma arrives at Station 7-Syd and acclimates to life on the farm. She meets the small crew: Viktor the proud old builder, Samuel the restless communications man, the silent Li, the twins Elias and Nora, and the watchful groundskeeper Karin. Cassia welcomes her with unsettling warmth — she treats Amma as an equal, which no one at Capitol ever did. Amma begins her covert inspection of module 7-12 while maintaining her cover as a replacement technician. Tal, a young newcomer who replaced Maja, shadows her suspiciously. Samuel drops observations that hit too close to the truth. Karin knows Amma's origins but protects the secret. Amma discovers the production anomaly in module 7-12 is real, recent, and unexplainable by standard technical failure. Maja was onto something. The act ends when Amma finds evidence that Maja's death was not accidental — and that someone on the station knows it.

## Act Two — Confrontation (Chapters 6–14)
Amma's investigation deepens as her cover begins to fray. Samuel confronts her about her lies. Tal's surveillance intensifies. Cassia draws Amma into confidence, sharing fragments about Maja and the "thirteenth foundation" — a hidden element of the station's infrastructure that doesn't appear in any official records. Amma and Cassia grow closer through shared labor, late conversations, and the physical intimacy of a harsh environment. But Amma's mission demands she report everything to Capitol, and what she's learning could destroy the station and everyone in it. The romance develops against a backdrop of mutual deception: Cassia is using Amma too, though her reasons are more complex than self-preservation.

### Midpoint
Amma discovers that module 7-12 contains something Capitol doesn't know about — a biological component that Maja found and was studying in secret. The thirteenth foundation is real. Cassia knew. This changes everything: Capitol sent Amma to confirm an accident, but what she's found is a secret worth killing for. Amma must decide whether to report the truth or bury it.

## Act Three — Resolution (Chapters 15–20)
Amma's loyalties fracture. Capitol presses for her report. Cassia reveals the full truth about the thirteenth foundation and what it means for the people beyond the Wall — Amma's people. The station community fractures as secrets surface. Samuel's desperation peaks. Viktor's health fails. Tal's motives become clear — he is Sol's son, and Sol died because of what he knew about the modules. The climax forces Amma to act: protect the station and its secret, or complete her mission and survive as a Capitol agent. She chooses, and the cost is real.

## The Ending
Amma transmits a false report to Capitol — the anomaly was a calibration error, nothing more. She stays at Station 7-Syd. But the lie is fragile. Capitol will send someone else eventually. Cassia and Amma face an uncertain future, bound by what they've hidden and what they've built. The Wall still stands. The war still burns. Nothing is solved. But something has been chosen.

## Thematic Statement
The most dangerous act of resistance is allowing yourself to belong to people you were sent to betray.
