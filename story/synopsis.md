# Synopsis

## Premise
Amma Lindqvist — born on the wrong side of the wall, smuggled across as an infant, forged into an undercover agent by Capitol's intelligence apparatus — arrives at Station 7-Syd, a remote solar farm on the sun-scorched frontier. Her mission: investigate a production anomaly at module 7-12, where the previous technician, Maja, died under suspicious circumstances. What she finds instead is a small, fractured community held together by its enigmatic commander, Cassia P. — a woman who treats Amma as an equal, sees too much, and asks her to report "anything strange" directly to her. As Amma's investigation uncovers truths about module 7-12 and "the thirteenth foundation," she must choose between the mission that was supposed to set her free and the people — one person in particular — who make her want to stay.

## The Hook
A woman who has lied about everything arrives at an isolated station where the last person who held her job died. The commander welcomes her warmly. Too warmly.

## Act One — Setup (Chapters 1–5)
Amma arrives at Station 7-Syd and meets the crew: Cassia (the commander who knows more than she reveals), Karin (the practical manager who immediately senses Amma's origins), Samuel (the chatty comms specialist who sees through facades), Viktor (the proud old-timer who found Maja's body), Li (the silent enigma), and the twins Elias and Nora. Amma establishes her cover as a replacement technician, begins learning the station's rhythms, and starts her covert investigation of module 7-12. Cassia draws her in with warmth and directness. Samuel needles her with observations that cut too close. Karin warns her to hide her origins. Chapter 2 introduces Tal, the volatile young newcomer who replaced Maja and who follows Amma with unsettling attention. By Act One's end, Amma has found her first anomaly at module 7-12 — the production data doesn't match the hardware — and Cassia has made a request that blurs the line between trust and manipulation: "Tell me first. Before you tell anyone."

## Act Two — Confrontation (Chapters 6–17)
Amma's dual life intensifies. Her investigation reveals that module 7-12's anomaly isn't a malfunction — it's producing something beyond standard solar energy, something connected to a classified project called "the thirteenth foundation." Maja found it first and died for it — or died because of it. Meanwhile, Amma's connection to Cassia deepens through shared vulnerability: late-night conversations, accidental proximity, the slow erosion of professional distance. Samuel confronts Amma about her lies, forcing her to reveal partial truths. Karin becomes a quiet ally. Viktor's health deteriorates. Tal's behavior grows more erratic — is he another agent? A grieving son? Both?

### Midpoint
Amma discovers that Cassia already knows about the thirteenth foundation — has known all along. Cassia isn't just a station commander; she was placed here to protect the secret. Everything Amma thought she understood about their relationship inverts. Cassia's warmth wasn't manipulation, but it wasn't innocent either. She was watching Amma as closely as Amma was watching her. The question becomes: watching for whom?

## Act Three — Resolution (Chapters 18–22)
Capitol sends orders: shut down module 7-12 and transfer the crew. The thirteenth foundation — whatever it produces — is to be buried. Amma must choose: complete her mission and return to Capitol with the intelligence, or help Cassia protect what Maja died to discover. The station fractures. Samuel's loyalty is tested. Tal reveals his father worked on the original thirteenth foundation project and died for it — his presence at the station was never coincidence. Viktor collapses. In the climax, Amma's cover is blown — not by an enemy, but by Samuel, who forces the truth into the open because he believes secrets are killing them. Amma and Cassia must face each other without pretense for the first time.

## The Ending
Amma chooses the station. She transmits false data to Capitol, buying time, knowing it won't last. She and Cassia stand together in the knowledge that what they've built — trust, intimacy, a fragile community — exists on borrowed time. The wall still stands. Capitol still controls. But module 7-12 keeps producing, and the people at Station 7-Syd keep choosing each other. It's not a happy ending. It's a beginning they'll have to fight for.

## Thematic Statement
The most dangerous thing you can do in a world built on division is to let someone know you — and to stay anyway.
