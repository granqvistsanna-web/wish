# Plot Plan

## Chapter Structure

**Target chapter count**: 20 chapters
**Target word count per chapter**: 3,000–5,000 words
**Target total word count**: 70,000–90,000 words

---

## Chapter Plan

### Chapter 1 — Arrival
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The road to Station 7-Syd, then the station itself. Late afternoon, brutal heat.
- **Purpose**: Establish Amma's cover, her psychology, and the station's atmosphere. Introduce the ensemble. Plant the mystery.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma arrives at Station 7-Syd. Karin meets her, recognizes she's from the wrong side of the Wall, and says nothing about it — except a quiet warning.
  2. Amma is shown the station: living quarters, kitchen, modules visible in the distance. Viktor boasts about building the place. Samuel talks too much and sees too clearly.
  3. First communal dinner — Amma observes the group dynamics. Li says nothing. The twins are curious. Cassia arrives late and changes the room.
  4. Cassia speaks with Amma privately. Welcomes her. Mentions Maja in passing — "she was good." Asks Amma to report anything unusual directly to her. The request feels wrong.
  5. Amma alone in her room. The shoulder aches. She counts ceiling tiles. She thinks about her mother crossing the Wall in the dark.
- **Emotional arc**: Guarded → overwhelmed by sensory overload → watchful → unsettled by Cassia's warmth.
- **Romance beat**: First meeting with Cassia. Amma registers Cassia's physical presence, her voice, how the room changes. Not attraction yet — awareness.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma notices Maja's coffee cup, untouched on the shelf. No one has moved it. No one has explained why.

---

### Chapter 2 — The Modules
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The energy modules, the fields around the station. Early morning, heat building.
- **Purpose**: Introduce Amma's technical work and the module mystery. Introduce Tal. Deepen the Cassia dynamic. Establish the physical reality of the farm.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma's first morning — adjusting to farm rhythms. Karin gives her coffee and instructions. Brief, practical.
  2. Amma walks to the modules for her first inspection. The landscape is vast, flat, punishing. She begins her technical assessment of modules 1 through 6 — routine, functional.
  3. Tal appears, following her. He's young, abrasive, Maja's replacement. He watches what Amma does at the modules. She's irritated. They exchange clipped words.
  4. Amma reaches module 7-12. Something is different — the readings don't match Capitol's records. The production anomaly is real. She makes notes she doesn't share.
  5. Cassia finds Amma at the end of the day. They walk back together. Cassia asks about the modules. Amma gives careful, partial truths. Cassia gives them back. They are circling each other.
  6. Tal is reprimanded by Cassia for abandoning his assigned work to follow Amma.
- **Emotional arc**: Focused, professional → irritated by Tal → genuinely unsettled by module 7-12 → carefully drawn to Cassia's company.
- **Romance beat**: The walk back with Cassia. Physical proximity. Shared silence that isn't uncomfortable. Amma notices Cassia's hands, the way she moves through the landscape like she belongs to it.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Back in her room, Amma compares her readings to Capitol's data. The numbers diverge sharply starting the week Maja died. Not before. After.

---

### Chapter 3 — The Communications Room
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The station's communications room, kitchen, Amma's quarters.
- **Purpose**: Establish Samuel as both threat and potential ally. Deepen the surveillance theme. Amma sends her first report to Capitol.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma needs access to the communications system to send her first report to Capitol.
  2. Samuel controls the comms room. He's chatty, probing. He reveals he knew Maja well — they arrived together, then she shut him out.
  3. Amma sends a minimal report: arrived safely, beginning assessment. She's careful, knowing Samuel can monitor transmissions.
  4. Samuel makes an observation that cuts: "You don't talk like a technician. You talk like someone who's been trained to talk like a technician."
  5. Evening — Viktor shares his illegal spirits. Stories about the early days. Amma lets her guard slip slightly.
- **Emotional arc**: Controlled → exposed by Samuel → retreat into professionalism → slight thaw with Viktor.
- **Romance beat**: Cassia absent this chapter — her absence noticed. Amma catches herself looking for her.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Samuel tells Amma that Maja used to send transmissions late at night. Unauthorized ones. He never reported it.

---

### Chapter 4 — Under the Sun
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The fields, module 7-12, the greenhouse.
- **Purpose**: Physical intimacy of shared labor. Amma's body betrays her. First real Cassia scene.
- **Key events**:
  1. Hard physical work in the heat. Amma's shoulder fails during a repair.
  2. Cassia helps her — matter-of-fact, physical, no fuss. The first real touch between them.
  3. In the greenhouse afterward, Cassia talks about her early days on the farm. Nightmares. Finding her place.
  4. Amma asks about Maja. Cassia's answer is careful: "She checked everything twice. Three times. I don't understand what happened."
  5. Karin finds them. A look passes between Karin and Cassia that Amma can't read.
- **Emotional arc**: Competent → vulnerable (shoulder) → disarmed by Cassia's care → suspicious again.
- **Romance beat**: First physical contact. Cassia's hands on Amma's shoulder. Professional but lingering.
- **Chapter-end hook**: That night, Amma goes back to module 7-12 alone. The readings have changed again since morning.

---

### Chapter 5 — The Other Side
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The station at night. Amma's memories. Module 7-12.
- **Purpose**: Amma's backstory. The Wall. Her mother. Why she became an agent. End of Act One.
- **Key events**:
  1. Insomnia. Amma's memories surface: crossing the Wall as a child, her mother's death, Sector 14.
  2. She finds Li awake in the kitchen, silent as always. They sit together without speaking. It's oddly peaceful.
  3. Next day: Amma's deeper analysis of module 7-12 reveals a biological signature in the energy readings. Something organic. Not mechanical failure.
  4. She realizes Maja found this too. That's what the late-night transmissions were about. Maja was reporting to someone — but not Capitol.
  5. Samuel confronts Amma: "You've been lying since you got here. I don't care about what. I care about why."
- **Emotional arc**: Haunted → briefly peaceful (Li) → intellectually electrified → cornered by Samuel.
- **Romance beat**: Amma thinks about Cassia while unable to sleep. The memory of her hands. She pushes the thought away.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma finds a maintenance log in Maja's handwriting, hidden inside module 7-12's access panel. The last entry reads: "It's alive."

---

### Chapter 6 — Trust
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Station 7-Syd, Cassia's office, the modules.
- **Purpose**: Act Two begins. Amma must choose whom to trust. First real scene between Amma and Cassia with stakes.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma brings Maja's log to Cassia — a calculated risk.
  2. Cassia's reaction reveals she already knew, or suspected. She asks Amma to keep this between them.
  3. Cassia mentions the "thirteenth foundation" for the first time. Doesn't explain.
  4. Tal catches Amma leaving Cassia's office. Tension.
- **Emotional arc**: Decisive → exposed → uncertain alliance.
- **Romance beat**: Behind closed doors. Cassia's voice drops. The intimacy of shared secrets.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Cassia: "Maja didn't die because of an electrical fault. But I need you to understand — the truth is worse."

---

### Chapter 7 — Foundations
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Module 7-12, underground access, the station.
- **Purpose**: The thirteenth foundation revealed. The mystery deepens.
- **Key events**:
  1. Cassia takes Amma below module 7-12. There's a thirteenth foundation — a hidden sub-level.
  2. What they find: a biological system integrated into the module's energy production. Something growing.
  3. Cassia explains: Maja discovered it. She believed it was the real power source. The modules above are just the interface.
  4. Amma's training kicks in — this is what Capitol would want to know. This is what could change the war.
  5. But Cassia asks her not to report it. "If Capitol knows, they'll strip this place to the bedrock."
- **Emotional arc**: Awe → scientific fascination → moral crisis.
- **Romance beat**: In the dark beneath the module, proximity becomes unbearable. They don't touch. The not-touching is the point.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma: "Who else knows?" Cassia: "Maja. Me. Now you."

---

### Chapter 8–14 — Rising Action
**Key developments across Act Two:**
- Amma and Cassia grow closer. Physical intimacy builds through shared work, proximity, stolen moments.
- Samuel discovers Amma's real role (or pieces it together). He doesn't expose her — he needs her.
- Viktor's health deteriorates. His tremors worsen. The farm is losing its institutional memory.
- Tal's surveillance of Amma intensifies. His father (Sol) died under similar circumstances to Maja — connected to module knowledge.
- Capitol sends increasingly urgent requests for Amma's full report. Her window is closing.
- Li breaks her silence — once, briefly, devastatingly. What she says changes Amma's understanding.
- The biological system beneath module 7-12 is growing. Changing. Amma realizes it responds to the people above it.
- Amma and Cassia's first real intimate scene: not sex first, but the moment when pretense drops entirely. Two liars telling the truth.

---

### Chapter 15–20 — Resolution
**Key developments across Act Three:**
- Capitol sends a second agent. Amma's time is up.
- Cassia reveals the full scope: the biological system could change the war — or end it. It could feed the people beyond the Wall.
- Amma's identity crisis peaks: she is Capitol's agent but a Wall-crosser by birth. She cannot serve both.
- The station community fractures when secrets emerge. Samuel's desperation. Tal's grief and rage.
- Viktor dies. The farm mourns.
- Climax: Amma must transmit her report. She writes two versions. She sends the false one.
- Denouement: Amma stays. Cassia and Amma face what they've chosen. The Wall still stands. But the seed is planted — literally and figuratively.
