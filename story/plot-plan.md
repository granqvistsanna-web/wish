# Plot Plan — TENESSA

## Chapter Structure

**Target chapter count**: 20
**Target word count per chapter**: 3,000–5,000 words
**Target total word count**: 60,000–100,000 words

---

## Chapter Plan

### Chapter 1 — Ankomst (Arrival)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Transport convoy → Station 7-Syd, first day
- **Purpose**: Establish Amma's cover, the farm's atmosphere, the weight of the frontier. The reader meets this world through professional eyes.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma arrives at Station 7-Syd. Karin processes her paperwork and establishes within minutes that Amma is from the wrong side of the border — says nothing, moves on.
  2. Viktor gives her the tour of the farm. Every section except modul 7-12.
- **Emotional arc**: Professional control. She's done this before. But the Wall is closer than expected and the silence is different here.
- **Romance beat**: First meeting with Samuel. He talks too much. She listens too carefully. They are immediately suspicious of each other.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Cassia, first evening, asks Amma to come to her directly if she finds anything unusual. *Before* filing a report.

---

### Chapter 2 — Inventering (Inventory)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Modul 7-12, panel fields, control room
- **Purpose**: Introduce the technical anomaly. Introduce Tal. Establish that the grid instability is more than equipment fatigue.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma reviews Sellen's archived work orders. The production discrepancy was logged after his death, not before.
  2. Tal follows Amma onto the panel field uninvited. She doesn't tell him to go back. She watches what he notices.
- **Emotional arc**: Professional focus shading into unease. Something here was covered up.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel works late in the communications room. They share bad coffee and silence. He asks one too many questions. She gives him one true answer by accident.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma confirms the discrepancy in the energy data. Production exceeds delivery. The difference is small, precise, and nightly.

---

### Chapter 3 — Tenessa
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Control room archives, Amma's quarters
- **Purpose**: Introduce the word. Trigger the mother connection. Establish personal stakes beneath the professional mission.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma finds TENESSA written repeatedly in Sellen's maintenance logs — flagged beside technical anomalies in precise, frightened handwriting.
  2. The word unlocks a physical memory: her mother's pendant, the engraved letters she used to trace as a child.
- **Emotional arc**: Professional → suddenly personal. Something old and buried surfaces without warning.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel notices she's stopped sleeping. He doesn't ask why. He leaves a food ration outside her door. Neither of them mentions it.
- **Chapter-end hook**: She retrieves her mother's notebook from her equipment case. The sketches inside. The margins.

---

### Chapter 4 — Noder (Nodes)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Network routing system, grid infrastructure, Cassia's office
- **Purpose**: Deepen the technical investigation. Show Cassia's information control.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma maps the routing system and finds a node that doesn't exist on official maps — TENESSA. It activates at 0300 on a precise schedule.
  2. Amma asks Cassia a direct question about the grid history. Cassia answers a different question elegantly and completely.
- **Emotional arc**: The investigation is not accidental. The system was designed to be invisible to routine maintenance.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel helps her trace a signal interference issue — legitimate work — and gets close enough to her workstation to see something he shouldn't. He doesn't react. She notices.
- **Chapter-end hook**: The cables feeding the TENESSA node run somewhere the building maps don't show.

---

### Chapter 5 — Förseglat (Sealed)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Inverter building exterior and corridors
- **Purpose**: First physical barrier. Establish that Sellen's access logs were falsified.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma attempts to access the sealed service corridor beneath the inverter building. A secondary lock requires authorization she doesn't have.
  2. Back in the control system: Sellen's credentials appear in the shaft's access log after his recorded time of death.
- **Emotional arc**: Frustrated → certain. This is not an accident. This is a murder with tidied-up edges.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel finds Amma at the sealed door late at night. Neither explains why they're there. They stand in the dark for a moment that lasts slightly too long.
- **Chapter-end hook**: The secondary lock uses an authorization class Amma doesn't have — but her handler might be able to get.

---

### Chapter 6 — Hanteraren (The Handler) — READER REVEAL ~25%
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Amma's quarters, covert transmission
- **Purpose**: THE REVEAL. The reader learns Amma is an undercover intelligence operative. Everything prior is reframed.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma opens a transmitter hidden inside a diagnostic calibration tool. She contacts her handler. The exchange is brief and controlled: mission confirmed, access codes delivered.
  2. The reader learns: intelligence detected anomalous energy patterns months ago. Sellen's death was the opening. Amma volunteered for the insertion. Her handler assumed professional ambition.
- **Emotional arc**: The reader's view of Amma shifts entirely. Her careful silence was tradecraft. Her precision was training. The personal history behind why she volunteered is still hidden.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel knocks on her door four minutes after she puts the transmitter away. She opens it. His expression says he heard nothing and knows everything is not fine.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Her handler's last line: *Do not let personal history compromise the mission.*

---

### Chapter 7 — Tuneln (The Tunnel)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Inverter building sealed shaft, underground corridor
- **Purpose**: Physical confirmation of the conspiracy. Establish the scale of what Tenessa is.
- **Key events**:
  1. Using the handler's access codes, Amma enters the sealed shaft beneath the inverter building. Old concrete. New cables. A tunnel that predates the solar farm by decades.
  2. The cables are new installations inside very old infrastructure — run toward the Wall with industrial precision.
- **Emotional arc**: Controlled methodology over rising dread. She keeps counting. Keeps recording.
- **Romance beat**: None. Mission only. This is Amma alone with the thing she came to find.
- **Chapter-end hook**: She maps the cable route on her private log. It runs directly toward the Wall's foundation — toward something the maps call nothing.

---

### Chapter 8 — Anteckningsboken (The Notebook)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Amma's quarters, late night
- **Purpose**: The mother connection confirmed. Personal stakes at maximum. Amma understands why she volunteered.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma compares her mother's notebook sketches — tunnel drawings, coordinate fragments, margin notes — with the infrastructure she found in the shaft. They match.
  2. Her mother was not preserving a memory of Tenessa. She was mapping its weaknesses. The pendant was a reminder of the thing that should never be used again.
- **Emotional arc**: Recognition that arrives slowly and then all at once. Her entire life has been a long consequence of a choice her mother made.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel brings her coffee at 0200 and sits outside in the corridor without asking to come in. She leaves the door open. He stays until 0400. They don't speak much. It's the most honest thing that's happened since she arrived.
- **Chapter-end hook**: The nightly diversion is getting larger. The machine is drawing more power each cycle. It's ramping up.

---

### Chapter 9 — Borrsystemet (The Drilling System) — MIDPOINT
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Control room, grid analysis
- **Purpose**: MIDPOINT. Tenessa is active. The machine is real. The scale of the threat is confirmed.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma maps the complete nightly energy diversion. The load profile matches industrial drilling equipment — a large machine already in operation beneath the Wall.
  2. She calculates the ramp-up trajectory. At current acceleration, the system reaches full operational capacity in weeks.
- **Emotional arc**: The theoretical becomes concrete. The engineering problem was never about power loss. It was always about what the power was feeding.
- **Romance beat**: For the first time, Amma tells Samuel something true about herself — not the cover, not a deflection. Not everything. But something. He goes quiet in a way that means he's paying attention.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Every authorization signature behind the power diversion routes through one office: *Halvar Ise, Regional Operations Director.*

---

### Chapter 10 — Ise
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Administrative complex (distant observation), control room, Cassia's evening rounds
- **Purpose**: Name and establish the antagonist. Show Cassia's ambiguity. Ise begins to notice Amma.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma identifies Halvar Ise: a calm, long-tenured administrator who has managed this facility and its predecessors for over two decades. She watches him from a distance.
  2. Amma asks Cassia a question that touches on the original infrastructure. Cassia answers without lying. She doesn't answer the question that was asked.
- **Emotional arc**: Controlled identification. She knows who. She doesn't yet know why.
- **Romance beat**: Amma and Samuel's first real argument — he pushes for something she won't give. She shuts the door. They both know the argument wasn't about what the argument was about.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Ise calls a routine staff review for the following week. Amma counts who looks at her when his name is mentioned.

---

### Chapter 11 — Stormen (The Storm)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Control room, during and after a desert dust storm
- **Purpose**: ROMANCE BEAT — intimacy through shared crisis. The Tenessa system runs through the storm, proving it's not weather-dependent.
- **Key events**:
  1. A severe dust storm disrupts power across the facility. Amma and Samuel work through the night together stabilizing the grid. Physical proximity, exhaustion, the specific honesty of crisis.
  2. The storm passes. The Tenessa diversion ran anyway, on schedule, uninterrupted. Whoever controls it was not afraid of the storm.
- **Emotional arc**: In the dark of the control room, Amma's guard slips. Not all the way. Enough.
- **Romance beat**: First kiss. Neither of them makes it significant in words. In the morning both of them know the situation is now more complicated.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Post-storm audit: one section of the panel field is reporting clean numbers that don't match the physical damage. Someone corrected the logs remotely.

---

### Chapter 12 — Konfrontation (Confrontation)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Communications room
- **Purpose**: Samuel catches her unauthorized network activity. Romantic rupture. His choice not to report is the first act of loyalty.
- **Key events**:
  1. Samuel finds evidence of Amma's unauthorized access — she was in system layers a technician has no business touching. He confronts her directly, not with bureaucratic language but with the specific anger of someone who's been lied to.
  2. He says: *Du har ljugit så länge att du har glömt vad som är sant.* He does not report it.
- **Emotional arc**: Amma: exposed, hollow. She has no argument. He's right. Samuel: angry in a way that is also grief — he had started trusting her.
- **Romance beat**: He withdraws. She lets him. The distance is the most honest thing between them — it means it mattered.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Why didn't he report it? She doesn't know. She should find out. She doesn't.

---

### Chapter 13 — Samuels val (Samuel's Choice)
- **POV**: Samuel (or Amma observing Samuel)
- **Setting**: Samuel's communications workspace, external relay station
- **Purpose**: Samuel's moral reckoning. His handlers. His decision not to serve them.
- **Key events**:
  1. Samuel's handlers make contact. They've detected anomalous energy readings near the Wall from their side. They want whatever he has. He has the Tenessa data.
  2. He realizes: they don't want to stop it. A destabilized border benefits both sides in certain ways. He is being asked to feed a machine he can choose not to feed.
- **Emotional arc**: The calculation he's avoided making finally arrives. He weighs it the way he weighs everything — then chooses the thing that costs him.
- **Romance beat**: He comes to find Amma. He doesn't explain what he's just given up. He says: *Tell me what you actually need.* It is a beginning.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Ise schedules a facility-wide security audit. One week.

---

### Chapter 14 — Ise på nära håll (Ise, Up Close)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Administrative complex, formal meeting
- **Purpose**: Establish Ise as a credible ideological threat. He is not a cartoon villain. His logic has weight.
- **Key events**:
  1. Ise summons Amma for a routine performance review — standard procedure, but his questions are not routine. He is measuring her.
  2. He speaks about the frontier with genuine patience. About systems that outlive their purpose. About the cost of keeping things frozen. He is charming and terrifying in equal measure.
- **Emotional arc**: Amma performs competence while unsettled by his logic. He is not wrong about the decay. He is wrong about the cure.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel watches the meeting from across the facility through a window he's not supposed to be near. He says nothing about it afterward. He doesn't have to.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma intercepts a scheduled power command buried deep in the control software — a final surge, timed, signed by Ise.

---

### Chapter 15 — Kommandot (The Command)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Control room (covert), then Amma's quarters
- **Purpose**: The clock starts. The final act begins. Amma tells Samuel everything.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma decodes the scheduled surge command. The Tenessa drilling system will reach full operational capacity and trigger in eleven days. She calculates what that means for the Wall.
  2. She tells Samuel everything: her real role, her mother, the pendant, the tunnels in the notebook. It is the first time she has told the full truth about herself to another person.
- **Emotional arc**: Urgency landing on something that feels like relief. She has been carrying this alone for a very long time.
- **Romance beat**: He doesn't leave. He doesn't reassure her. He says: *Then we have eleven days.* It is the most romantic thing he has said.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Samuel: *There's a physical junction beneath panel row 9. That's where the routing switch is.*

---

### Chapter 16 — Sellen och Ise (Sellen and Ise)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Archived communications logs
- **Purpose**: Close the murder investigation. Confirm the killing chain.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma recovers archived internal messages showing Sellen contacted Ise directly about the energy anomaly. He was not threatening anyone — he was doing his job.
  2. The timestamp: Sellen's message was sent at 14:23. His death was logged at 17:08. He was killed the same afternoon.
- **Emotional arc**: Clarity with a cold edge. The murder was not complicated. It was efficient.
- **Romance beat**: Amma and Samuel plan together in practical terms. They are functional and precise with each other in a way that is its own kind of intimacy.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Security audit moved up. Thirty-six hours.

---

### Chapter 17 — Vad Cassia vet (What Cassia Knows)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Cassia's office, after hours
- **Purpose**: Cassia's moral position resolved — not villain, not innocent. A person who knew what she could afford to know.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma confronts Cassia directly. Cassia admits she suspected Sellen's death was not an accident. She was told, obliquely, to stop asking. She has workers to protect.
  2. Cassia tells Amma where the physical junction for the routing switch is located — a piece of information Amma needs and Cassia has been sitting on for months.
- **Emotional arc**: Amma: anger that cools into something complicated. Cassia protected what she could. It wasn't enough. It was also not nothing.
- **Romance beat**: None — purely plot terrain. But Cassia looks at Amma the way people look at someone who has already decided.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Cassia: *If you're going to do something, do it before the audit. After that, they'll have current system maps.*

---

### Chapter 18 — Konfrontation med Ise (Confrontation with Ise)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Administrative complex, Ise's office
- **Purpose**: The ideological confrontation. Ise's argument. His offer. Amma's answer.
- **Key events**:
  1. Ise meets Amma directly. He has known she was not just a technician for some time. He lays out the Tenessa logic: the stalemate is civilizational decay. The system must be broken before both sides collapse from within.
  2. He offers her a choice: walk away, report what she found to her handlers (knowing what they'll do with it), or disappear like Sellen. He is calm throughout.
- **Emotional arc**: Amma holds the shape of herself in a room designed to dissolve it. She has her mother's face in her head. The pendant in her pocket.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel is in position at the routing junction. Amma knows this. It is the only thing that keeps her voice level.
- **Chapter-end hook**: She tells Ise she needs twenty-four hours to decide. He believes her. He shouldn't.

---

### Chapter 19 — Sabotaget (The Sabotage)
- **POV**: Amma and Samuel (alternating or close third)
- **Setting**: Panel field, routing junction, control room
- **Purpose**: The act. The cost. The silence after.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma and Samuel cut the routing switch at the physical junction. The power diversion to the Tenessa network drops to zero. The drilling system loses power.
  2. The control room goes dark for forty seconds before backup systems engage. Forty seconds of silence beneath the Wall.
- **Emotional arc**: Controlled action over terror. Then: it's done. The silence after is enormous.
- **Romance beat**: In the dark after — not a kiss, not a declaration. Just the specific weight of two people standing close in a room where they've done something that cannot be undone.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Ise's authorization in the system goes silent. Then an external access attempt. Then nothing. He has already left the facility.

---

### Chapter 20 — Gryningen (The Dawn)
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Solar field, dawn
- **Purpose**: Resolution without resolution. The cost. The thematic close.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma's handler contacts her at 0500. The official report will describe an equipment failure in the grid that was corrected. No mention of Tenessa. Sellen's death will remain an accident in the record.
  2. Amma sends one more transmission — not to her handler. To the intelligence unit on the other side of the Wall that Samuel's people use. It contains everything. The full technical record of Tenessa.
- **Emotional arc**: Not relief. Not victory. The specific exhaustion of someone who has done the thing they came to do and knows the world is not changed by it — only slightly less wrong.
- **Romance beat**: Samuel and Amma, dawn light, opposite sides of the control room. The Wall still stands. Neither of them has anywhere straightforward to go. Neither makes a promise. Both made the same choice.
- **Chapter-end hook**: None. The sun rises over the panel field. The towers along the Wall blink on schedule.

## Notes on Structure

- **The 25% reveal** (Ch. 6): The reader discovers Amma is an undercover agent. Everything prior is reframed — her silence, her precision, her observation of people.
- **Midpoint** (Ch. 9): Tenessa is confirmed active. The story shifts from investigation to countdown.
- **Romantic rupture** (Ch. 12): Samuel catches Amma. The relationship goes dark before it can deepen.
- **Mutual commitment** (Ch. 15): Amma tells Samuel the full truth. The alliance becomes personal.
- **Ideological confrontation** (Ch. 18): Ise states his case. He is wrong but not stupid. This must land.
- **No clean ending**: The Wall stands. Identities remain precarious. The choice they made was right. The world didn't notice.
