# Plot Plan

## Chapter Structure

**Target chapter count**: 22 chapters
**Target word count per chapter**: 3,000–5,000 words
**Target total word count**: 80,000–100,000 words

---

## Chapter Plan

### Chapter 1 — Arrival
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The road to Station 7-Syd; the station exterior; communal dining room (first dinner)
- **Purpose**: Establish Amma's cover, her interior conflict, the station's isolated world, and the core cast. Plant Maja's absence as a ghost in every detail.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma arrives at Station 7-Syd after a long, uncomfortable journey. She approaches on foot for the final stretch — heat, dust, the farm rising from flat terrain.
  2. Karin meets her at the gate. Immediate, terse assessment. Shows her the station in clipped commands. Amma notices the absent "assistant button" on Karin.
  3. Communal dinner: Amma meets the crew — Viktor (boastful, hard of hearing, trembling hands), Samuel (chatty, probing, the scar), the twins Elias and Nora (silent, watchful), Li (completely silent, eats methodically, looks up only when Cassia enters).
  4. Cassia arrives late to dinner. The room shifts. She greets Amma with warmth that feels genuine and calculated simultaneously. She asks Amma about her journey, her background — Amma deploys "safe truths."
  5. After dinner, Cassia walks Amma to her quarters. Mentions Maja obliquely — "The last technician. She was good." Asks Amma to report anything unusual about module 7-12 to her first. Amma agrees, unsettled.
  6. Alone in her room, Amma inventories: the narrow bed, Maja's scratches on the wall by the headboard, the shoulder that aches against the thin mattress. She counts her breaths. She is here. The mission has begun.
- **Emotional arc**: Guarded control → sensory overwhelm of new place → careful social navigation → unsettled by Cassia's request → alone with the weight of what she's pretending to be.
- **Romance beat**: First meeting with Cassia. Amma registers Cassia's physical presence before she registers her words. The attraction is immediate and unwelcome.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma lies in Maja's old bed, fingers tracing scratch marks on the wall she doesn't understand yet. She counts them. Twelve marks. Then a gap. Then one more.

---

### Chapter 2 — The Field
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The solar modules, the field, module 7-12 exterior
- **Purpose**: Introduce Amma's work at the station, introduce Tal, establish module 7-12's strangeness.
- **Key events**:
  1. First day of work. Amma inspects modules with routine checks.
  2. Tal appears — follows her to the field. Uncomfortable, probing presence.
  3. Amma reaches module 7-12. Something about the readings doesn't match what she expected.
  4. Tal and Amma return; Cassia reprimands Tal for abandoning his duties.
- **Emotional arc**: Professional competence → unease with Tal → first whisper of the anomaly → frustration at being watched.
- **Romance beat**: Amma catches herself looking for Cassia at breakfast. Notices the absence.
- **Chapter-end hook**: The production data from module 7-12 shows output that shouldn't be possible from the hardware installed.

---

### Chapter 3 — Safe Truths
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Communications room, greenhouse, communal spaces
- **Purpose**: Deepen Samuel and Amma's dynamic. Samuel sees through her. Introduce Viktor's still and his stories.
- **Key events**:
  1. Samuel shows Amma the communications system — the thing that could expose her if she's careless.
  2. Samuel asks pointed questions about her background. His observations cut close.
  3. Viktor invites Amma to see "his farm" — a tour that's really a monologue about what he built. Behind the greenhouse: the still.
  4. Karin pulls Amma aside. "I wouldn't mention where you're from. Not everyone here would understand."
- **Emotional arc**: Tension with Samuel → comic relief with Viktor → a moment of genuine vulnerability when Karin protects her.
- **Romance beat**: Cassia finds Amma alone after Karin's warning. A brief, warm exchange. Cassia's hand on the doorframe, close but not touching.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Samuel, watching Amma leave the comm room: "You've been lying so long you've forgotten what's true." He says it like a joke. It isn't.

---

### Chapter 4 — The Cup
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Dining room, Amma's quarters, module 7-12 interior
- **Purpose**: Maja's ghost becomes concrete. Amma enters module 7-12 for the first time. The anomaly deepens.
- **Key events**:
  1. At breakfast, Amma reaches for a cup. Silence falls. It was Maja's.
  2. Amma enters module 7-12's interior for detailed inspection. The hardware has been modified — not broken, but deliberately altered.
  3. She finds something Maja left behind — a notebook? Markings? Data scratched into a surface?
  4. She reports to Cassia — but not everything. She holds something back.
- **Emotional arc**: Social misstep with the cup → professional focus in the module → the thrill and fear of discovery → the first deliberate lie to Cassia.
- **Romance beat**: Reporting to Cassia in her office. Close quarters. Amma notices Cassia's hands, the way she listens with her whole body. The lie sits between them.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Back in her room, Amma matches Maja's wall scratches to the modifications in module 7-12. Twelve modules. A gap. Then one more. The thirteenth.

---

### Chapter 5 — The Thirteenth
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Station at night, module 7-12, Cassia's quarters
- **Purpose**: End of Act One. Amma names the mystery. Her relationship with Cassia crosses a line from professional to personal.
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma sneaks to module 7-12 at night to investigate further.
  2. She discovers the module is producing an energy signature that doesn't match solar — something else is happening underground or within the foundation.
  3. Tal catches her. Tense confrontation — but he backs down, says nothing.
  4. Amma goes to Cassia. "What is the thirteenth foundation?" Cassia's face changes. "Tell me what you found." Not a denial. Not surprise. Recognition.
  5. Cassia asks: "Tell me first. Before you tell anyone."
- **Emotional arc**: Fear and adrenaline → discovery → confrontation with Tal → the terrifying intimacy of asking Cassia a question that reveals how much she knows.
- **Romance beat**: The conversation in Cassia's quarters at night. The first moment where the power dynamic shifts — Amma has information Cassia needs. They are, briefly, equals.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Cassia says, "Maja asked me the same question." She pauses. "I should have answered her."

---

### Chapter 6–10 — Act Two, First Half
Rising investigation. Amma digs deeper into module 7-12 and the thirteenth foundation. Romance with Cassia builds through late-night conversations, shared silences, accidental touch. Samuel becomes both threat and confidant. Viktor's health declines. Tal's volatility increases. Li remains an enigma but Amma notices patterns — Li is always near module 7-12 at specific times.

### Chapter 11–14 — Midpoint and Fallout
Amma discovers Cassia has known all along. Trust shatters. Amma retreats. The station feels the tension. Samuel forces a confrontation. Cassia reveals partial truth: she was sent to protect the thirteenth foundation, not exploit it. Maja discovered it independently and Cassia failed to protect her.

### Chapter 15–17 — Act Two, Second Half
Amma and Cassia rebuild trust on new terms — honest ones. They work together to understand the thirteenth foundation. Their relationship becomes physical. Capitol sends a communication that changes everything: module 7-12 is to be decommissioned.

### Chapter 18–20 — Act Three, Escalation
Capitol dispatches an inspection team. Amma must decide: agent or ally. The crew learns truths about each other. Tal reveals his father's connection. Samuel's loyalty is tested. Viktor has a medical crisis.

### Chapter 21 — The Break
Samuel blows Amma's cover — not maliciously, but because he believes secrets are what killed Maja. Everything comes out. The crew must decide what they are to each other now that the lies are stripped away.

### Chapter 22 — The Thirteenth Foundation
Amma transmits false data to Capitol. She and Cassia stand together, knowing it won't hold. The station endures. The wall still stands. But the people at Station 7-Syd have chosen each other, and that choice is its own kind of foundation. Not the thirteenth. The first.
