# Plot Plan

## Chapter Structure

**Target chapter count**: 20 chapters
**Target word count per chapter**: 3,000–5,000 words
**Target total word count**: 70,000–90,000 words

---

## Chapter Plan

### Chapter 1 — Arrival
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The transport vehicle, then Station 7-South exterior and kitchen
- **Purpose**: Establish Amma's cover, introduce the station and the first characters (Karin, Viktor), plant the mystery of Maja's death
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma arrives at the station after a long transport through the desert. Karin meets her.
  2. Karin gives her a terse tour. Viktor brags about building the place.
  3. First meal in the communal kitchen — Amma observes the dynamics. Maja's empty chair/unused coffee cup noticed.
  4. Karin identifies that Amma is from "the wrong side of the Wall" and warns her to keep it quiet.
- **Emotional arc**: Controlled fear → cautious observation → the first crack (Karin's warning rattles her)
- **Romance beat**: None yet. Establishing isolation.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Karin tells Amma the last technician died at the module she'll be inspecting tomorrow.

---

### Chapter 2 — The Crew
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Station 7-South — communal dinner, the grounds, the module field
- **Purpose**: Introduce remaining characters (Cassia, Samuel, Li, Tal, twins), deepen the mystery, establish Amma's inspection mission
- **Key events**:
  1. Cassia arrives and transforms the room. Dinner scene — all characters present, voices established.
  2. Samuel talks too much, observes too closely. Viktor's hearing creates comic relief.
  3. Li says nothing. Tal is introduced — hostile, young, Maja's replacement.
  4. Cassia takes Amma aside and asks her to report anything unusual to Cassia first, before Capitol.
  5. Tal follows Amma outside, watching her. Cassia scolds him for leaving his work.
- **Emotional arc**: Guarded performance → disarmed by Cassia's warmth → unsettled by Tal's hostility
- **Romance beat**: First spark — Cassia's attention, or Samuel seeing through Amma. The pull of being known.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma lies in bed, shoulder aching. She can hear someone walking the perimeter in the dark. She counts the steps. They stop outside her window.

---

### Chapter 3 — Module 7-12
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: The module field, Module 7-12, the communications room
- **Purpose**: Amma begins her actual inspection, discovers the first anomaly, Samuel confronts her
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma walks out to the module field at dawn. The scale and heat of the landscape.
  2. She inspects Module 7-12 — the official production anomaly doesn't match what she finds. Something has been physically altered.
  3. She documents what she can, but needs access to the communications system logs to cross-reference.
  4. Samuel catches her in the communications room. He's sharp beneath the chatter. He tells her he knows she's lying about who she is.
  5. A tense exchange — Amma deflects, Samuel pushes. He doesn't expose her, but the trust is conditional.
- **Emotional arc**: Professional focus → growing unease at what she finds → fear when Samuel confronts her → fragile, uneasy truce
- **Romance beat**: Samuel and Amma alone for the first time. Vulnerability through confrontation. He sees her, and she hates it. And doesn't.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Back at the module, Amma finds a mark Maja scratched into the panel — a number. Thirteen.

---

### Chapter 4 — The Assistant
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Station grounds, greenhouse, Karin's quarters
- **Purpose**: Explore the assistant buttons, deepen Karin relationship, introduce the thirteenth foundation concept
- **Key events**:
  1. Amma is given her assistant button. She notices who wears theirs and who doesn't (Karin refuses).
  2. Work scenes — the physical reality of maintaining the modules. Heat, dust, exhaustion.
  3. Amma asks Karin about the assistant buttons. Karin's answer is clipped and evasive.
  4. Viktor tells a story about building the station that accidentally references the "thirteenth foundation" — a foundation they poured but were told to bury.
- **Emotional arc**: Curiosity → physical grounding in the work → frustration at evasion → spark of understanding
- **Romance beat**: Amma watches Cassia with the crew and sees genuine care. It confuses her mission.
- **Chapter-end hook**: Amma checks the station blueprints. There are only twelve module foundations on the official plans.

---

### Chapter 5 — Rain
- **POV**: Amma
- **Setting**: Station 7-South during a rare rainstorm
- **Purpose**: Force intimacy through proximity (everyone trapped inside), Samuel backstory, Amma's PTSD triggered
- **Key events**:
  1. An unexpected storm traps everyone indoors. The station feels smaller.
  2. Samuel tells Amma about the North Coast, rain, his siblings. First genuine conversation.
  3. Thunder triggers Amma's PTSD from Sector 14. She freezes. Samuel notices.
  4. He doesn't ask. He puts his hand on her shoulder. She lets him.
- **Emotional arc**: Restless confinement → lowered guards → vulnerability → the cost of being touched
- **Romance beat**: The shoulder touch. Physical contact that means something. Not romantic yet — survival-intimate.
- **Chapter-end hook**: The storm clears. Amma goes outside. Module 7-12's readings have changed — the rain triggered something.

---

### Chapter 6–10 — [Investigation Deepens]
- Investigation of the thirteenth foundation
- Amma and Samuel develop trust through shared work at the communications system
- Cassia's protectiveness becomes more complex — is she protecting the crew or the secret?
- Tal's hostility peaks — confrontation about Maja
- Li does something unexpected that changes Amma's understanding
- Amma sends partial reports to Capitol, withholding what she's finding

### Chapter 11–14 — [Midpoint Crisis]
- Amma discovers the thirteenth foundation's purpose
- Romance reaches intimacy — physical and emotional
- Amma's loyalty splits openly
- Someone discovers her true identity

### Chapter 15–18 — [Escalation]
- Capitol sends follow-up team
- Station crew fractures
- Romance crisis — betrayal revealed
- Amma must choose sides

### Chapter 19–20 — [Resolution]
- Final confrontation
- Amma destroys her cover to protect the station
- Costly, imperfect ending
- The farm endures. Amma stays. Trust rebuilds slowly.
