## 1. Score: 8.5/10

Strong chapter. The character work is largely excellent — Amma's interiority is precise, Samuel's scene-closing confrontation is the best writing in the draft, and Li's wordless module scene does exactly what the bible asks. The deductions come from Viktor losing his defining comedic texture almost entirely, and one over-explicit moment in Samuel's voice that flattens the dynamic he is supposed to embody.

## 2. Amma

**Consistent with bible: Yes, with one note.**

Amma's counting instinct is present and grounded from the first paragraph. She wakes before dawn because the body forces her, works the axel-skada into the narration as a practical matter rather than pathos: "Värker längs ärrvävnaden — fukten i luften, Viktor hade haft rätt om det." That is exactly the bible's formulation: *kroppen är ett verktyg. Hon ignorerar smärta tills den tvingar sig på henne.*

The counting is threaded throughout with discipline. Lamporna längs norrsidan: *elva i rad. Sedan ett mellanrum. Sedan tre till.* Takplankor at the close: *sexton. Sexton igen.* These are not decorative tics — they function as structural brackets, opening the chapter on the building's geometry and closing it on sleeplessness and threat. The counting is doing its work.

The "safe truths" strategy is more implied than explicit this chapter, which is the right calibration for chapter two. She does not volunteer information; she deflects with protocol ("Inspektionsprotokoll") and offers nothing personal except functional competence. The mint plant carries the safe-truth function obliquely — she waters it, she associates it with Maja, but she never explains what the mint means to her. That restraint is earned.

Her internal log-keeping — *Antecknade i bakhuvudet: Frågade om 7-12 omedelbart. Accepterade avböjning utan utmaning. Vill veta om jag prioriterar den — inte om jag vill ha hjälp* — is a strong realization of how her mind works under cover. Analytical, not emotional. She reads everyone as a variable.

**One note**: The final line of her post-Samuel reflection — *Det var mer som att någon lade ner ett kort på bordet och drog tillbaka handen utan att visa resten av handen* — reads slightly too metaphorical for Amma's register. Her internal voice elsewhere is almost entirely concrete: data, observation, gap between variables. This is the one place she reaches for an analogy, and it is a fairly common one. It is not wrong, but it is softer than her usual precision.

## 3. Samuel

**Largely correct. One line to fix.**

Samuel's bible gives explicit behavioral guidance: *han pratar mest när han inte vill bli läst. När han blir tyst är det ett tecken på att han ser något farligt.* Chapter 2 executes this well in two out of three scenes.

The breakfast scene is excellent. He arrives unexpectedly quiet — the draft flags this: *Han var oväntat tyst för att vara den han var — ingen mening halvfärdig i munnen, ingen gest inåt rummet.* That opening deviation from his default pattern signals to Amma (and the reader) that something is operational. He observes, asks the single most precise question available ("Du börjar inte med tolv?"), accepts the deflection without pushing, then fills the remaining time with weather data — exactly the bible's formulation of talking as cover. *Han rörde sig mot fönstret och pratade sedan om vädersystemet norr om stationen i fem minuter, med en detaljnivå som antydde att han kände sig hemma i ämnet och gärna stannade där.* This is right.

The module 6 scene is also strong. His route through the module — *rutten han gick var rutten en person gick för att se varje del av modulens arbetsyta utan att verka titta* — is shown rather than explained, which is the correct approach. The internal number-slip, referring to 7-12 as "sjutton," is a fine detail. It implies informal familiarity, a different relationship to the module than his professional role requires.

**The line to fix**: In the room scene, Samuel says: *"Det vet jag inte. Men jag vet vad hon hittade. Och jag vet att du inte är här för allmänt underhåll. Ingen är."* The final sentence — *Ingen är* — is over-explicit. Samuel's bible says his questions "låter slarviga men är extremt precisa." This line does not sound like precision disguised as sloppiness; it sounds like a character explaining the scene's subtext. Everything before it ("Jag vet vad hon hittade," "Du är inte här för allmänt underhåll") is economical and correctly weighted. *Ingen är* crosses into commentary and briefly makes Samuel sound like a plot mechanism rather than a person. The line reads as if he is clarifying the story's premise to the reader, not doing something tactical in the room.

His closing gesture — *Några veckor. Inte råd. Bara information* — is the chapter's best beat and belongs entirely to his voice.

## 4. Cassia

**Brief but accurate.**

Cassia appears twice: a passing mention at dinner and the reference to her first-evening instruction — *Kom till mig. Innan du lämnar in rapporten.* The dinner scene gives her exactly what the bible prescribes: *ett ansikte som är svårt att sluta titta på* translated into a behavioral beat: *En gång, under en paus mellan Viktors meningar, tittade hon direkt på Amma. Inte länge. Två sekunder. Den sortens blick som betyder jag noterar vad du gör.*

That is Cassia. The "fully present" eyes in the bible become a timed surveillance in the text. Her function in this chapter is to remain a pressure point — the person Amma must decide whether to trust — and the draft keeps her ambiguous without abandoning her. Her physical presence (she does not dominate the dinner conversation; Samuel does) correctly subordinates her screen time without erasing her weight.

No inconsistencies with bible.

## 5. Viktor

**Present but stripped of his defining trait.**

Viktor's bible is built around two pillars: the storytelling pride (*kärnsträvan: stolthet, berättande*) and the bad hearing (*hör dåligt — komiska missförstånd*). The draft captures the first fully and drops the second almost entirely.

The module 3 scene is Viktor at his best. *Han berättade om modul tre med ägarens precision — vilken skarv han svetsade, vilken koppling som bytts ut den vintern det blåste hårdare än väderprognosen sagt.* His hand flat against the module wall — *inte en gest, bara en landning* — is a precise physical detail that communicates his relationship to the place without stating it. The Maja moment (*Han pratar om henne i presens*) is the chapter's sharpest piece of grief writing.

But the bible specifically calls out the hearing gag as a character-defining texture: *Amma: "Åtta år." Viktor: "Två?"* This mechanism serves two purposes — comedy that humanizes Viktor before darker content, and a signal that he may not always fully receive what is being said to him (which has plot utility). In chapter 2, the exchange *"Du vet din sak." / "Åtta år."* passes cleanly. Viktor hears it correctly, responds warmly, and moves on. There is no misfire, no comic loop. This is a missed opportunity to anchor his distinctive trait in the chapter and a minor inconsistency with the established characterization.

His grief for Maja — the presens tense observation, the hand on the wall — does read right. That element is handled with care.

## 6. Li

**Her silence is established as a choice, not an absence. This works.**

Li's bible says: *tystnad och osynlighet i en grupp som annars är öppen.* The module 4 scene earns this characterization fully by showing Li in action rather than in social context. She is working; she is methodical; she does not look up when Amma arrives. When she extends her hand to hold the cable housing, it is an observation-driven act — she saw the problem before Amma asked. Then: *Gick tillbaka till sin sektion.*

Amma's analysis of what Li's movement economy communicates is the chapter's most perceptive character-reading passage: *Den sortens rörelseekonomisering kom antingen från lång vana vid trånga utrymmen eller från en vana vid att inte bli sedd. Möjligen bägge.* This is correct to both Li's mystery and to how Amma's mind works. Amma reads bodies and gaps.

The crucial line is: *Det kändes inte som likgiltighet. Det kändes som ett val — att vara i rummet utan att vara tillgänglig för det.* This is exactly what the bible asks the chapter to establish. Li's silence is active, not passive. It is exercised.

Her absence from dinner (*Li var frånvarande*) is noted without explanation, which keeps her correctly in the register of the unexplained.

No inconsistencies. Li functions as the bible intends.

## 7. Character Inconsistencies

**Two issues, one minor, one moderate.**

**Moderate — Viktor's hearing**: As noted above, his hearing impairment never surfaces in chapter 2. Given that the bible specifies it as a comic and practical trait (*hör dåligt — komiska missförstånd*) and it is deployed in chapter 1, its complete absence here registers as a gap. Viktor is in several scenes with direct dialogue. The trait should surface at least once.

**Minor — Samuel's "Ingen är"**: Already flagged in section 3. This is a voice inconsistency more than a character inconsistency, but Samuel's bible is specific about how he conceals sharpness: through imprecision and indirection. "Ingen är" is pointed and direct in a way that does not match how he operates.

## 8. Relationship Dynamics: Amma/Samuel Tension

**Building correctly. Pacing is right for chapter 2.**

The tension operates on two tracks and the draft maintains both.

Track one is operational: Amma assessing Samuel as a potential threat (the surveillance-officer sweep of his gaze, her log entry: *Samuel. Primär funktion kan vara surveyans snarare än kommunikationsteknik*). This is the professional layer, and it is working.

Track two is the recognition dynamic the bible specifies: *han känner igen något i henne innan han förstår vad.* The breakfast scene positions this without naming it — his question about module 7-12 is too fast and too precise for casual conversation, and he accepts her deflection in a way that communicates he recognized it as a deflection rather than a real answer. The space between his question and his acceptance (*Nicken var ett erkännande av spelreglerna snarare än ett godkännande av dem*) is exactly the right register for where they are in chapter 2.

The room scene escalates the recognition to something more explicit but keeps it operational. Samuel does not ask about her feelings or her cover. He offers a data point about Maja — precisely calibrated — and leaves. The chapter's final beat, Amma sitting with *Det var mer som att någon lade ner ett kort på bordet*, frames the escalation without overclaiming romantic content yet. That is the right pacing.

What is not yet present, and should not be in chapter 2, is the physical dimension. The bible describes a romance arc that turns at midpoint. Chapter 2 correctly keeps the tension intellectual and operational. The heat is potential energy, not kinetic. That is appropriate.

## 9. Top 3 Character Moments That Work

**1. Samuel's room-scene closing: "Några veckor. Inte råd. Bara information."**

This is the chapter's best single beat. The editorial voice inside the narration — *inte råd. bara information* — captures Samuel's precise relationship to what he just said. He is not warning her. He is choosing how much to reveal, and the framing makes that distinction structural. The fact that Maja searched for weeks and then died, delivered without affect, is exactly the kind of pressure the story needs at this stage. It functions because Samuel's economy of speech in this scene earns it.

**2. Li in module 4: the extended hand.**

*En gång, när Amma behövde hålla ett kabelcasinglock på plats med en hand och använda instrumentet med den andra, sträckte Li ut handen och höll höljet. Amma hade inte bett om det.* This is a masterclass in characterization through action. Li's intelligence about the workspace, her decision to help, and her immediate return to silence after the single "tack" communicates more about who she is than any exposition could. It also establishes the mutual-assessment quality of their coexistence: two people deciding independently that the other is not an immediate problem.

**3. Viktor's hand on the module wall.**

*Hans händer lade sig platt mot modulens plåtvägg — inte en gest, bara en landning.* The distinction between gest and landning is precisely observed. This is Viktor's body speaking his attachment to the place without Viktor being aware he is doing it. It sets up the presens-tense Maja observation that follows and makes Viktor's grief legible through physical vocabulary rather than statement.

## 10. Top 3 Character Moments to Fix

**1. Samuel: "Ingen är" (room scene, near end)**

The line — *"Det vet jag inte. Men jag vet vad hon hittade. Och jag vet att du inte är här för allmänt underhåll. Ingen är."* — needs its final sentence cut or replaced. "Ingen är" makes Samuel a spokesperson for the chapter's subtext. His value is in the slant approach: questions that sound slack but land precisely, information dropped without framing. A possible direction (without rewriting): the observation should be implicit in the weight of the previous two sentences, or redirected into something operational — a fact about the station rather than a meta-comment about why people come to it.

**2. Amma's card-on-table metaphor (post-room reflection)**

*Det var mer som att någon lade ner ett kort på bordet och drog tillbaka handen utan att visa resten av handen.* Amma's internal register throughout the chapter is concrete, numerical, architectural. She counts lamps, photographs oxidation, maps access delays in seconds. This metaphor is softer and more conventional than her elsewhere voice. The insight it is reaching for — Samuel offering just enough to confirm without revealing — is correct, but Amma's version of it should probably be more structural: a gap in data, a calibrated omission, something technical. The romantic framing (the card game metaphor carries a low-level intimacy) arrives a scene too early.

**3. Viktor's hearing not surfacing (module 3 and 5 scenes)**

Viktor appears in substantial scenes — the shared inspection, modul 3 berättelsen, the breakfast avoidance of certain modules — and never mishears anything. His bible is explicit about this trait as characterization. The *"Åtta år" / "Du vet din sak"* exchange is the natural moment for the mishear loop, and it passes clean. One misfire — even short — would anchor his established voice and provide tonal relief before the chapter's darker second half. Its absence makes Viktor feel flatter than chapter 1 established him to be.

# Chapter 2 — Stilgranskning

**Reviewer**: Style Agent
**Score: 8.5/10**

## Key findings

**Clean on forbidden words**: Zero violations. No AI filler vocabulary.

**Sentence structure**: Strong. Short declarative punches ("Underifrån." / "Inget fel." / "Sömnen kom inte.") earn their weight. One pattern to watch: the data analysis block (production anomaly) runs seven consecutive noun-subject sentences at near-identical lengths. The post-Samuel analysis block repeats "Han hade inte... Inte... Inte..." across too many lines without structural break.

**Em-dashes**: Slightly high density in technical sections — no single page violation but accumulates visually.

**Sensory grounding**: Met and exceeded throughout. Shoulder ache, corrosion detail, temperature differential in 7-12, pen wear pattern, mint plant as structural motif. Weak spot: dinner scene thin on physical sensation (no smell, texture, light quality).

**Dialogue tags**: Correct — "sa han/sa Amma" throughout, no adverb tags.

**Emotional register**: Earned. No melodrama. "Amma lade ner stiftet. Myntplantan behövde vatten. Hon reste sig, fyllde ett glas vid kranen, gav den en exakt mängd." is cited as the chapter's best Butler-grade deflection.

## Top 3 passages to revise

**1. "datum point" + follow-through explanation (post-room reflection):**
> "Det sättet att förhålla sig till en annan person — inte varning, inte tystnad, utan ett enstaka datum point lämnat löst — var ett sätt hon inte riktigt hade ett bra namn på. Det var inte neutralt. Det var inte allians."
- "datum point" is a Swedish/English code-switch artifact
- "Det var inte neutralt. Det var inte allians." tells the reader what the scene already showed
- Cut the explanatory follow-through

**2. "precis tillräckligt för att bekräfta" (post-room reflection):**
> "Han hade sagt precis tillräckligt för att bekräfta att det hon tittade på var verkligt — och tillräckligt för att påminna henne om att Maja sökte i veckor och sedan dog."
- This is a plot-summary sentence, not a consciousness sentence
- The inventory of what Samuel withheld is strong; the "precis tillräckligt" clause needs to go or change register

**3. "Vad man lämnade efter sig..." (closing Maja meditation):**
> "Vad man lämnade efter sig var ofta inte det man trodde. Inte rapporter eller anteckningar eller loggar, utan pennan i ett filterhölje. Myntan i ett fönster."
- The opening thesis announces the subtext instead of letting it land silently
- Cut "Vad man lämnade efter sig var ofta inte det man trodde." — start directly with "Inte rapporter eller anteckningar eller loggar..."

## Top 3 passages working well

**1. The *Sjutton* sequence (modul 6):**
> "Nummer. Inte '7-12', inte 'modulen'. *Sjutton.* Det var vad de kallade den internt, möjligen, eller det var vad han kallade den."
Double-register writing: behavioral observation + linguistic analysis in one beat. The italicized *Sjutton.* as a single-word paragraph is exactly the right rhythm choice.

**2. Amma's production anomaly response:**
> "Amma lade ner stiftet. Myntplantan behövde vatten. Hon reste sig, fyllde ett glas vid kranen, gav den en exakt mängd."
"Exakt" carries the weight — both in-character and quietly devastating. Three short sentences, no explanation.

**3. Viktor's module wall landing:**
> "Hans händer lade sig platt mot modulens plåtvägg — inte en gest, bara en landning."
The distinction between gest and landning is precisely observed. Grief through the body's muscle memory.
