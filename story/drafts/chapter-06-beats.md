# Chapter 6 — Lojaliteter (Loyalties)

## POV
Amma

## Setting
Station 7-Syd. Communications room, Cassia's office, the compound perimeter near the wall. Dusk and night.

## Purpose
The consequences of chapter 5 arrive. Amma must choose between her mission (report to Capitol) and what she's discovered. Cassia reveals she knows about the fundament — and has been protecting it. Tal's role becomes clearer: he's not just Maja's replacement, he's someone's eyes. The chapter ends with Amma making a choice that changes everything.

## Beats

### Beat 1 — The Report Window
Amma has a scheduled check-in with her handler. She sits in the communications room (Samuel's domain — he's not here, she checked). The encrypted channel is ready. She types her report: anomalous energy readings, module 7-12, possible biological contamination. Her finger hovers over SEND. She thinks about what Samuel said. She deletes "biological contamination" and types "equipment malfunction under investigation." She sends a nothing-report. She's just bought herself — and the fundament — maybe a week.

### Beat 2 — Cassia Summons
Cassia asks Amma to walk with her. Not a request. They walk the perimeter path, the wall visible in the distance — a dark ridge against the sunset. Cassia is quiet for a long time. Then: "Maja hittade det efter fyra år. Du hittade det efter fyra dagar. Du är antingen mycket bättre eller mycket mer desperat." *Maja found it after four years. You found it after four days. You're either much better or much more desperate.*

### Beat 3 — Cassia's Truth
Cassia knows about the thirteenth fundament. Has known since before she became station chief. The reason her "meritlista" seems too long for a remote posting: she *chose* this assignment. She's been protecting the fundament — from Capitol, from the military, from anyone who would exploit it. The farm's purpose isn't just energy production. It's camouflage. "Farmen existerar för att dölja det som finns under den." *The farm exists to hide what's beneath it.* Amma: "Vad är det?" *What is it?* Cassia: "Jag vet inte. Men det har funnits här längre än muren. Längre än Capitol. Kanske längre än oss."

### Beat 4 — The Real Question
Cassia asks Amma the question that matters: "Vem skickade dig egentligen?" *Who really sent you?* Amma's cover is intact — technically — but Cassia isn't asking about her cover. She's asking about loyalty. Amma could lie. She's been lying for years. Instead she says: "Energidepartementets inspektionsenhet." The truth. Cassia nods slowly. "Och vad har du rapporterat?" *And what have you reported?* Amma: "Ingenting som spelar roll. Än." *Nothing that matters. Yet.* The "yet" hangs between them. Cassia: "Bra. Håll det så."

### Beat 5 — Tal Confrontation
Returning to the compound, Amma finds Tal outside her quarters. He's been inside — she can tell from the dust pattern on the door. He's searched her room. He doesn't bother hiding it. "Var är anteckningsboken?" *Where's the notebook?* Amma (she's carrying it on her body): "Vilken anteckningsbok?" *What notebook?* Tal steps close. He's younger than her but there's something behind his eyes that isn't young at all. "Min far dog för att han ställde frågor om det trettonde fundamentet. Maja dog för samma sak. Sluta gräva." *My father died because he asked questions about the thirteenth fundament. Maja died for the same thing. Stop digging.*

### Beat 6 — The Revelation
Tal's father — Sol/Solen — worked on the farm before Viktor. He was the one who discovered the fundament during construction. He reported it. He died. Viktor, who poured the concrete, knows this. Karin knows. Cassia knows. Tal came here not as Maja's replacement but to find out what his father found — and who killed him for it. Tal and Amma are doing the same thing from different angles. But Tal doesn't trust her. "Du är från Capitol. Capitols folk dödar. Det är vad ni gör." *You're from Capitol. Capitol people kill. That's what you do.* He leaves.

### Beat 7 — Night, Alone
Amma sits on her bed. Maja's notebook open. Her false report sent. Cassia's secret shared. Tal's accusation burning. She is: an agent who has lied to her handlers. A woman born on the wrong side of the wall pretending to be from the right side. Investigating a death that might have been ordered by the people she works for. Protecting something alive that she doesn't understand. She presses her palm against the floor. Twenty meters below, does the fundament feel her? She thinks it does.

### Beat 8 — Closing Image
She reaches for her assistentknapp. For a moment she considers removing it — like Karin, like Li. Going dark. Instead, she leaves it on. She'll need it to keep sending false reports. To buy time. She lies down. The floor is warm — warmer than it should be. She sleeps with her hand flat against it, counting heartbeats that might not be her own.

## Emotional Arc
Amma: Tension (the report) → calculated deception → vulnerability with Cassia → shock from Tal → isolation → a fragile, dangerous peace. She's chosen a side. She just can't name it yet.

## Romance Beat
Not Amma/Samuel directly — but Amma/Cassia's scene carries intimate energy. Trust as the most dangerous form of closeness. Amma's growing connection is to the *place* and its people, not just one person. The romance is still being earned.

## Chapter-End Hook
Amma is now lying to Capitol. Tal's father and Maja were killed for what they knew. Amma now knows the same thing. And her biometrics are being watched.
