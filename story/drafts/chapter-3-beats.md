# Chapter 3 Beat Sheet — "Module 7-12"

**POV**: Amma (close third person, past tense)
**Timeline**: Day 2, dawn through evening
**Setting**: Module field, Module 7-12 interior, communications room, station kitchen
**Word target**: 3,000–5,000

---

## Beats

### Beat 1 — Dawn Walk (Opening)
**What happens**: Amma wakes before the others. Shoulder stiff from sleeping on the thin mattress. She walks out to the module field at first light — the air still cool, the sand pale grey. The scale of the landscape hits her: rows of modules extending to the horizon, heat already gathering at the edges. She counts her steps. An old habit.
**Emotional undercurrent**: Controlled purpose. This is why she's here. The social complexity of the station falls away when she has a technical task.
**Sensory anchor**: Cold sand through boot soles. The metallic smell of the modules before the sun heats them. Her breath visible in the pre-dawn air.
**Dialogue**: None. Interior monologue only.

### Beat 2 — Module 7-12 Exterior
**What happens**: Amma reaches Module 7-12. It looks like the others from outside — squat, rectangular, solar panels angled south. But the access panel has been opened and resealed. Not by a maintenance crew — the sealant is wrong. She photographs it with her datapad. The official report said electrical malfunction in the control panel. She hasn't opened the panel yet and already knows something is off.
**Emotional undercurrent**: Professional sharpening. This is what she's trained for — reading the wrong kind of normal.
**Sensory anchor**: The sealant's texture under her fingertips — too smooth, commercial grade, not the rough station-issue compound. The sun climbing, heat starting to press.

### Beat 3 — Inside the Module
**What happens**: Amma opens the access panel and enters the module's service corridor. Cramped, dark, humming. She runs her inspection: power readings, connection points, circuit boards. The production anomaly in the official report doesn't match what she sees. Output hasn't dropped — it's been redirected. A bypass has been installed. Someone rerouted energy from the main grid into a secondary circuit that isn't on any blueprint she was given.
**Emotional undercurrent**: Growing unease. This isn't a malfunction. This is intentional. And it's competent work — Maja's work.
**Sensory anchor**: The hum of the module vibrating through her chest. Heat building in the enclosed space. Sweat at her hairline. The smell of heated circuits and dust.
**Dialogue note**: Amma mutters to herself while working — brief, technical. "That's not right." "Where does this go?"

### Beat 4 — The Scratch Mark
**What happens**: Deep inside the module, behind a junction box, Amma finds a scratch in the metal panel. Not random damage — deliberate. A number scratched with a tool: 13. Below it, smaller, a series of letters or coordinates she can't fully read in the dim light. She photographs it. Her hands are not entirely steady.
**Emotional undercurrent**: The shift from "inspection" to "investigation." This is evidence. Maja left a mark.
**Sensory anchor**: The scratch under her fingertip — the metal's raw edge where the coating was scraped away. The insufficient light. The heat now unbearable.

### Beat 5 — Return to the Station (Transition)
**What happens**: Amma emerges from the module into blinding light. The heat is brutal now — midmorning. She walks back toward the station, processing what she found. She needs the communications system logs to cross-reference the bypass's installation date. She needs to know when Maja made those modifications and whether anyone else knew.
**Emotional undercurrent**: The mission just changed. She came to file a report on a production anomaly. This is something else.
**Sensory anchor**: The sun on her neck like a hand pressing. Sweat cooling on her back. The station visible as a low cluster of buildings wavering in the heat.

### Beat 6 — The Communications Room
**What happens**: Amma enters the communications room to access the system logs. The room is small, cluttered with Samuel's things — a shirt hung on a hook, coffee cups, loose papers. She starts pulling up maintenance logs for Module 7-12. She finds records of Maja's access — dozens of entries over the weeks before her death. Increasing frequency. Then nothing.
**Emotional undercurrent**: Immersion in data. Amma is good at this — reading patterns, finding what's missing. But she's in Samuel's territory.
**Sensory anchor**: The communications room is cooler, darker. Screens casting blue light. Samuel's smell — coffee and unwashed cotton. The click of keys.

### Beat 7 — Samuel Catches Her
**What happens**: Samuel appears in the doorway. He's been awake — or he heard her. He doesn't shout or accuse. He leans against the doorframe and watches her for a moment before speaking. "You're up early." The conversation starts light — his usual chattiness. But then he narrows. He asks what she's looking for. She deflects. He pushes. Then, quietly: "You've lied so long you've forgotten what's true." Not an accusation. An observation. Almost sad.
**Emotional undercurrent**: Caught. But also — seen. The terror and the relief of it happening simultaneously.
**Sensory anchor**: His voice low in the small room. The door still open behind him. She can feel the distance between them — four steps, maybe five.
**Dialogue notes**: Samuel's voice shifts from chatty to sharp. Amma's responses shorten — she's calculating, not performing. Key line from Samuel: "You've lied so long you've forgotten what's true." Amma's response: silence. Then a deflection that doesn't quite land.

### Beat 8 — The Truce
**What happens**: Samuel doesn't expose her. Instead, he makes a choice: he tells her that Maja used the communications system constantly in her last weeks. Accessing files he didn't recognize. Technical specifications that weren't part of normal maintenance. He didn't report it because Maja told him not to. He asks Amma: "What did you find at the module?" She weighs her options. She gives him one piece of truth — the bypass. Not the scratch. Not the number thirteen. One piece, to see what he does with it.
**Emotional undercurrent**: A fragile negotiation. Neither trusts the other. But they're both holding pieces of a puzzle and they know it.
**Sensory anchor**: Samuel sits down across from her. The distance halved. She notices his scar — the pale line from neck to shoulder, visible where his collar shifts. His hands on the table, open.

### Beat 9 — Samuel's Piece
**What happens**: Samuel shares something in return. Maja stopped talking to him three weeks before she died. But the night before her death, she came to the communications room. Late. She asked him to send a message — to someone outside, not through official channels. He refused. Said it was too risky. She left without arguing. She was dead the next afternoon. He's carried this since. The guilt is quiet but total.
**Emotional undercurrent**: Grief and guilt surfacing. Samuel has been waiting to tell someone. Amma is the first person who might understand why it matters.
**Sensory anchor**: Samuel's voice changes — flatter, slower. He stops making jokes. His hands go still for the first time since she's known him.

### Beat 10 — Alone Again
**What happens**: Amma leaves the communications room. She and Samuel have no explicit agreement, but the truce is understood: he won't expose her, she won't report his failure to flag Maja's activity. They both know more than they're saying. Amma walks to the kitchen. Karin is there, arms crossed. "Eat," Karin says. Amma sits. She eats. The food is real and solid and she is hungry and she cannot remember the last time she noticed.
**Emotional undercurrent**: The performance slipping. The body's needs overriding the mind's control. She is tired and shaken and for the first time, she is not entirely alone in what she knows.
**Sensory anchor**: The food — bread, some kind of preserved fruit, water that tastes of mineral. Karin's presence, solid and demanding nothing except that Amma eat.

### Beat 11 — The Mark (Closing)
**What happens**: Evening. Amma reviews the photos on her datapad in her quarters. She zooms in on the scratch inside Module 7-12. The number thirteen is clear. Below it, now visible in the enhanced image: not coordinates. Letters. A word. She reads it twice and sits back. It's a name she recognizes. It shouldn't be scratched inside a solar module on a farm in the middle of nowhere.
**Emotional undercurrent**: The ground shifting. What she thought was a simple investigation just connected to something much larger.
**Sensory anchor**: The blue light of the datapad in the dark room. Her shoulder throbbing. The footsteps on the perimeter again — same time as last night. She doesn't go to the window.

## Romance Progression
First real one-on-one between Amma and Samuel. The confrontation is the intimacy — being seen through your lies is a form of nakedness. The truce they reach is not trust but the precursor to it. Physical details: his scar, his still hands, the distance between them in a small room.

## Thematic Thread
Lies and truth. Who do you tell the truth to, and what does it cost? Maja told no one and died. Samuel kept Maja's secret and it destroyed him quietly. Amma shares one truth with Samuel and it changes both of them.

## Chapter-End Image
The blue light of the datapad. A name scratched in metal. Footsteps outside that Amma has decided not to investigate — not yet.

## State Changes to Track
- Amma now knows: Module 7-12 has been intentionally modified (energy bypass). Maja scratched "13" and a name inside the module. Maja used the communications system to access unknown files before her death.
- Samuel now knows: Amma found something at the module (the bypass — not the scratch). Amma is not who she says she is.
- Samuel has revealed: Maja asked him to send an unauthorized message the night before she died. He refused.
- Relationship shift: Amma ↔ Samuel moved from wariness to fragile, conditional truce.
- Relationship shift: Amma ↔ Karin — small but real moment of care (eating together).
