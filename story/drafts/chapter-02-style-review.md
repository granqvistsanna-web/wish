## 4. Sentence Variety

**Average sentence length**: 9.2 words (below the 12–18 target range)
**Range**: 1–43 words

**NEEDS ATTENTION.** The average skews short. This is partly a consequence of the Rooney-influenced style (short declarative observations, clipped interiority), and partly because of the heavy dialogue, which naturally runs short. However, the narration also tends toward short-to-medium sentences. The chapter could benefit from a few more complex, longer sentences in the descriptive/reflective passages to vary the rhythm.

**Specific flag — three consecutive single-word sentences** (line 117):
> *Pulserade. Tyst. Envist.*

This is technically a violation of the "no 3 consecutive same-length" rule. However, it works as a deliberate rhythmic choice at the chapter's close — a pulse, a breath, a refusal to stop. **Recommend keeping it** but flagging it as an intentional exception.

## 7. AI-Sounding Prose

**No significant flags.** The prose reads as genuinely authored. A few observations:

- The voice is consistent and human throughout. The interiority never over-explains or becomes pedagogical.
- No "as if" constructions are overused (there are a few, but they feel natural).
- The metaphors are earned and specific rather than generic.

**One minor flag** — Line 75:
> *"det var inte det sortens ögonblick som romaner beskriver"*

This is a meta-narrative comment (the text commenting on its own genre). It works here because it deflates romantic cliché, which is very Rooney. But it's the kind of move that can feel writerly if overused. Track this — if similar meta-comments appear in later chapters, it will start to feel like a tic.

## 8. Repetitive Patterns

**Minor flags:**

- **"som om" (as if)** appears in lines 3, 19, 87 (x2), 109. Five instances in a 1,600-word chapter is on the high side. Consider cutting one or two, especially in line 87 where it appears twice in the same paragraph (*"som om den placerats där med avsikt. Som om någon ville..."*). The second one could be restructured.

- **Skruven (the screw):** Samuel's screw appears on lines 23, 27, 33, 67, 107. This is clearly an intentional character detail (fidgeting, nervous energy), and it works. But five mentions may be one too many. Consider cutting the instance on line 67 — it's the least narratively useful.

- **"Amma visste/kände" (Amma knew/recognized):** Lines 25, 73, 111. Three instances of Amma's knowledge/recognition as an interiority move. Not a problem yet, but worth watching in future chapters.

## 9. Sally Rooney Style Assessment

**This chapter succeeds as Rooney-influenced prose.** Specific strengths:

- **Conversational interiority**: Amma's thought process feels natural, associative, honest. Line 49 is a perfect example — the narration unpacks a question the way a mind actually would, circling around what makes it strange. *"Vad gör folk på kvällarna. Inte: hur ser byggnaderna ut... Utan kvällarna. Vad folk gör med sin tid."*

- **Dialogue as mutual performance**: The conversation between Amma and Samuel reads like two people negotiating how much to reveal. Neither says what they mean directly. Samuel talks too much as a strategy; Amma gives "something safe" (line 55) rather than truth. This is classic Rooney territory.

- **Emotional precision without sentimentality**: Line 75 — *"Det var bara kort och stilla och ärligt, som att de båda stod i samma rum utan att låtsas att rummet var något annat än vad det var."* This is excellent. It describes connection without romanticizing it.

- **Class and status as lived texture**: Samuel's Capitol backstory (lines 61–71) emerges naturally through dialogue, not exposition. The detail about "ett jobb med egen toalett" (a job with its own toilet) is perfectly chosen — specific, material, revealing.

**One area where the Rooney influence could go further**: Rooney often lets her characters be wrong about each other in ways the reader can detect. Amma's observations about Samuel are mostly accurate (he's observant, he talks as strategy). Consider adding a moment where Amma misreads him — it would add complexity and make her fallible as a narrator.
