Either Samuel is echoing what others have said (plausible), or this should be noted for the character reviewer. Not a timeline error per se.

### POTENTIAL ISSUE — Maja's assistentknapp:

The detail that the knapp was found "på kroken vid dörren" and not on her body is presented as suspicious. This is new information introduced in this chapter (not in any existing timeline or character sheet). It does not contradict anything established, but it should be **logged in the timeline** going forward as a key clue.

### POTENTIAL ISSUE — Duration at Capitol:
- Line 73: "Hon hade räknat steg i två år i Capitol innan hon fick det här uppdraget." This means Amma spent 2 years in Capitol before this mission. Her cover says "sju år" at the Energy Department. The character sheet says she has been at the Energy Department for 7 years as her cover. If she only spent 2 years in Capitol, the 7-year claim is part of her cover/legend (a lie), which is consistent with her being an undercover agent. However, this could also be read as: she spent 2 of her 7 years just counting steps in corridors before getting this specific assignment — meaning the 7 years is real but this particular field mission came after 5 years of other work + 2 years of corridor-walking. **This is ambiguous but not contradictory.** Worth clarifying in state files.
