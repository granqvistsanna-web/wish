# Chapter 4 — Assistenten (The Assistant)

## POV
Amma

## Setting
Station 7-Syd. Amma's quarters, the common room, the greenhouse (behind which Viktor distills). Night into early morning.

## Purpose
The "assistentknapp" (assistant button) is explored — what it does, who wears it, who refuses. Amma decodes the first pages of Maja's notebook. Karin reveals she knows more about Amma's origins than she should. The farm's social fabric tightens around Amma.

## Beats

### Beat 1 — The Notebook, Night
Amma in her quarters, reading Maja's notebook by dim light. Technical shorthand she can only half-decode. Diagrams of the module's substructure — the "fundament" system beneath the solar collectors. Maja was mapping something underground. Notes become increasingly frantic. Recurring phrase: "Det trettonde fundamentet svarar" — *The thirteenth fundament responds.* Responds to what?

### Beat 2 — The Button
Morning. Amma examines her own assistentknapp — standard Capitol-issue biometric monitor, worn under the collar. Tracks vitals, location, stress levels. Everyone wears one. Except Karin. And Li. Amma wonders what they know. She puts hers on — she has to, it's part of her cover — but she notices the twins touching theirs absently, like a tic. Viktor's is half-hidden. Samuel's status is unclear.

### Beat 3 — Breakfast Dynamics
Common room. Amma observes the group more carefully now. Li eats in silence as always, but Amma catches her watching — not the food, not the others, but the *walls*. As if listening. The twins share a look when Cassia mentions the module inspection schedule. Viktor tells a long, rambling story about building the original foundations. He says "tretton fundament" — thirteen foundations — for the first time out loud. "Twelve for the modules. And one more. Don't ask me what the thirteenth is for. I just poured the concrete."

### Beat 4 — Karin Corners Her
Karin catches Amma alone in the corridor. Short, direct: "Du läser Majas anteckningar." *You're reading Maja's notes.* Not a question. Amma freezes. Karin: "Jag hittade dem först. Lade tillbaka dem." *I found them first. Put them back.* A beat. Then: "Vad tror du hände med henne?" *What do you think happened to her?* Amma: "Jag vet inte än." *I don't know yet.* Karin studies her. "Bra. Fortsätt inte veta ett tag till." *Good. Keep not knowing for a while longer.* She walks away.

### Beat 5 — The Greenhouse
Amma can't sleep. Walks the compound at night. Finds Viktor behind the greenhouse with his still. He's drunk, sentimental. Less guarded. He talks about Maja — "Hon var inte rädd för någonting. Förutom det där sista." *She wasn't afraid of anything. Except that last thing.* What last thing? Viktor shakes his head. He can't or won't say. But he grips Amma's arm: "Det trettonde fundamentet. Låt det vara." *The thirteenth fundament. Leave it alone.* His hands are trembling worse than usual.

### Beat 6 — Tal in the Dark
Walking back, Amma sees a light at module 7-12. Someone is inside. She approaches. It's Tal. He's standing at the control panel, not working — just standing, one hand flat on the surface. He doesn't see her. His lips are moving. When he finally notices her, his face closes like a door. "Vad gör du här?" *What are you doing here?* She asks the same. He pushes past her without answering.

### Beat 7 — Closing Image
Back in her quarters, Amma turns to the last page of the notebook she can read. Maja wrote: "Fundamentet är inte betong. Det lever. Och det vet att jag vet." *The fundament isn't concrete. It's alive. And it knows that I know.*

## Emotional Arc
Amma: Methodical investigation → growing unease as warnings accumulate → genuine fear at Maja's final entry. The farm is not what it appears. Her mission is not what she thought.

## Romance Beat
Still pre-romance. But Karin's protective warning and Viktor's drunken plea both read as forms of intimacy — people trusting Amma with fragments of truth. The ground is being prepared.

## Chapter-End Hook
The fundament is alive. Maja knew. And Maja is dead.
