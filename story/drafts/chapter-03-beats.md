# Chapter 3 — Modulen (The Module)

## POV
Amma

## Setting
Station 7-Syd. Module 7-12 interior. Late afternoon heat.

## Purpose
Amma gets her first real look at the module where Maja died. The technical anomaly becomes concrete. Samuel reveals himself as more observant than his chatter suggests.

## Beats

### Beat 1 — Assignment
Cassia assigns Amma to inspect module 7-12. Matter-of-fact tone, but she watches Amma's reaction. "Maja's module. You'll want to start there." Amma notes the phrasing — *want*, not *need*.

### Beat 2 — The Walk Out
Amma crosses the compound alone. Heat. Dust. The farm's scale becomes clear — rows of solar collectors stretching toward the wall, which is visible as a dark line on the horizon. She counts her steps (coping mechanism). Her shoulder aches.

### Beat 3 — Inside the Module
Module 7-12 is different from the others. The control panel has been repaired — new wiring over old burns. The scorch marks where Maja died are still visible on the floor, painted over but not well enough. Amma crouches, runs her fingers along the seam. Her hands are shaking. She forces them still.

### Beat 4 — The Anomaly
Amma pulls up the production logs. Module 7-12's output has been *increasing* since Maja's death — not decreasing. This makes no sense. A damaged module should underperform. She cross-references with the other twelve modules. 7-12 is now producing 40% more energy than any other module. The "production anomaly" in the report wasn't a drop — it was a spike.

### Beat 5 — Samuel Appears
Samuel shows up with tools and excuses — "Karin sent me to check the comm relay." He's lying. There is no comm relay in this module. He lingers. Asks casual questions that aren't casual: "Maja spent a lot of time here. Did they tell you what she was working on?" Amma deflects. Samuel's chatter drops away for a moment and his eyes go sharp. "You're not really here to fix anything, are you?"

### Beat 6 — The Deflection
Amma gives him an "ofarlig sanning" (harmless truth): she admits the module's numbers don't add up and she was sent to find out why. Half-truth. Samuel accepts it — or pretends to. He tells her Maja stopped talking to him three weeks before she died. "She found something in here. And then she stopped being Maja."

### Beat 7 — Closing Image
Amma alone in the module after Samuel leaves. She finds something wedged behind the control panel — a small notebook, handwritten. Maja's. The last entry is a sketch: thirteen rectangles in a row, twelve the same size and one — the thirteenth — drawn larger, with roots extending downward. Below it, one word: *Fundament*.

## Emotional Arc
Amma: Professional detachment → unease → genuine fear when she realizes the anomaly is the opposite of what she expected → unsettled intimacy with Samuel's sharpness → dread at finding Maja's notebook.

## Romance Beat
No romance yet — but the Amma/Samuel dynamic shifts. He drops his mask briefly. She sees him. This is the seed.

## Chapter-End Hook
The thirteenth fundament. What did Maja find beneath the module?
