# Chapter 3 Reviews

## Style Review — Score: 7/10

### CRITICAL
1. **Dialogue ratio too low**: 18.3% (target 30–45%). The first half of the chapter is almost entirely narration. The Samuel confrontation scene carries the dialogue but needs expansion, and additional dialogue beats should be added elsewhere.
2. **Em-dash overuse**: 3.3 per page (max 2 per page). 42 em-dashes across ~12.6 pages. Many can be replaced with commas, periods, or restructured sentences.

### MAJOR
3. **Adverb density too high**: 1 per 137 words (target: 1 per 200+). 23 adverbs total. Key offenders: "perfectly," "defensively," "constantly," "methodically," "faintly," "exactly" (×3).
4. **"Landscape" on line 71**: "The landscape swam" — borderline metaphorical use. Replace with a more concrete noun.

### MINOR
5. **Paragraph starts**: "She" starts 21/116 paragraphs (18%). While no 3+ consecutive runs were found, the density is high. Vary opening constructions.
6. **Sensory grounding in dialogue sections**: The Samuel confrontation scene (section 5) has the lowest sensory density. Add body/environment details during the long exchange.
7. **No forbidden words detected** beyond the "landscape" flag.
8. **No exclamation marks in narration** — clean.
9. **Dialogue tags**: "Said" usage is compliant (>80%).

## Character Review — Score: 8/10

### CRITICAL
None.

### MAJOR
1. **Samuel's voice flattens in second half**: After the truce, his characteristic chatter, self-irony, and associative speech disappear. He becomes uniformly somber for too long. Break up his exposition with verbal tics — jokes, tangents, self-deprecation.
2. **Amma-Samuel truce forms too easily**: The transition from confrontation to alliance at the scene break (line 151) is too smooth. Samuel should resist more. Add a beat where the truce almost fails.

### MINOR
3. **Samuel's physical restlessness absent before grief scene**: He should fidget/shift during the confrontation, so his stillness during the Maja confession lands harder by contrast.
4. **Amma's reaction to the name (end of chapter) lacks sensory specificity**: Needs a physical/memory flash connected to her origin — not a named emotion, but a sensory fragment.
5. **Samuel checking access codes feels unprepared by chapter 2**: Frame as routine curiosity, not deliberate investigation.
6. **Samuel's prejudice contradiction missing**: His disdain for people from the other side of the Wall would complicate Amma's trust.
