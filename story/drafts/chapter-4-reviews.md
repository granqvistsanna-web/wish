# Chapter 4 Reviews

## Style Review — 6.5/10

### Key Issues
1. **Dialogue too sparse** (3.6% vs 20-60% target). Extend Samuel scene or add third conversational encounter. Give conversations intellectual/political substance.
2. **Em-dash overuse** (~4/page vs 2/page max). Reduce by 50%. Convert to commas, periods, subordinate clauses.
3. **Sentence rhythm too short** (avg 12.4 words vs 14-22 target). Need more long comma-spliced analytical sentences.
4. **"Det krävde" anaphora** (line 49) — four-beat parallel construction reads as AI pattern. Vary syntax.
5. **"Hon skrev inte" triple anaphora** — violates forbidden pattern (3+ paragraphs same word). Rewrite.
6. **"Hon tänkte på" triple attribution** — drop "hon tänkte" markers per style guide. Just state the thoughts.
7. **"Den sortens" appears 8+ times** — overused construction, conspicuous.
8. **"Inte X, inte exakt — snarare Y" construction** appears too often. Vary.
9. **Lines 53-54**: "Hon var inte rädd / hon var säker" — too essayistic. Let certainty emerge through action.
10. **"Noden var inte slumpmässig. Noden var punktlig."** — too polished chiasmus for interior thought.
11. **No intellectual/political content in dialogue** (0% vs 30% target).

### What Works
- Cassia meeting scene is excellent craft
- FID generally strong, filters through Amma's analytical mind
- Short declaratives land well ("Det pekade mot ingenstans")
- Sensory details well-rationed (4, within 3-5 target)
