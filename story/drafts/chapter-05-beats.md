# Chapter 5 — Svar (Response)

## POV
Amma

## Setting
Station 7-Syd. Module 7-12 substructure. The fundament level — beneath the solar farm. Midday heat above, cool below.

## Purpose
Amma descends beneath module 7-12 and encounters the thirteenth fundament directly. The sci-fi premise crystallizes: the farm is built on something biological, something that responds to human presence. The Amma/Samuel connection deepens under pressure. Amma must decide whether to report what she's found — and to whom.

## Beats

### Beat 1 — Decision
Morning. Amma weighs her options. She should report to her handler in Capitol — anomalous energy readings, dead technician, possible biological contamination. That's the professional move. Instead, she pockets a flashlight and a multitool and heads for module 7-12. She tells herself it's reconnaissance. She knows it's more.

### Beat 2 — The Access Hatch
Beneath the module's floor panels, Amma finds the maintenance hatch Maja must have used. It's not locked — the lock has been *removed*, recently, clean tool marks. Below: a ladder into darkness. The air rising from below is warm and wet, wrong for a concrete foundation in desert heat. It smells organic — soil after rain, something growing.

### Beat 3 — The Descent
Amma climbs down. Her shoulder screams. She counts rungs (coping). The shaft is deeper than a foundation should be — ten meters, fifteen, twenty. The walls change texture. Concrete gives way to something else: smooth, warm, slightly yielding. Not stone. Not metal. When she presses her palm flat against it, she feels a pulse. Faint. Slow. Like a heartbeat at rest.

### Beat 4 — The Thirteenth Fundament
The shaft opens into a chamber. The space is vast — wrong for what should be under a solar module. The floor is the same living material as the walls. Bioluminescence, faint blue-green, pulses in slow waves. At the center: a structure that rises from the floor like a column or a trunk, thick as three people, surface textured like bark or skin. It's warm. The air hums at a frequency Amma feels in her teeth. Her assistentknapp buzzes against her collarbone — the biometric readings must be spiking.

### Beat 5 — Contact
Amma touches the structure. The response is immediate. The bioluminescence flares. The hum shifts pitch — lower, resonant, she feels it in her bones. And then: a sensation she can't name. Not sound, not touch. Something reaching back. Recognition. The thing *knows* she's there. Not like a sensor — like a mind. She yanks her hand back. The light fades slowly, reluctantly, like an eye closing.

### Beat 6 — Samuel, Again
Amma climbs out shaking. Samuel is waiting at the module entrance. This time he doesn't pretend. "Du gick ner." *You went down.* He's pale. He knows about the chamber. "Maja visade mig. Två veckor innan hon dog." *Maja showed me. Two weeks before she died.* He looks at Amma's hand — the one she touched the structure with. "Kände du det?" *Did you feel it?* She nods. His jaw tightens. "Maja kände det också. Och sedan kunde hon inte sluta gå ner."

### Beat 7 — The Choice
Samuel asks what Amma is going to do. Report it? To whom? Capitol will send a team. They'll excavate. They'll weaponize it or destroy it. "Det är det enda levande jag har sett på sex år som inte försöker döda någon." *It's the only living thing I've seen in six years that isn't trying to kill someone.* This is the most honest Samuel has been. His voice is stripped of its usual deflection. Amma feels the pull — toward him, toward the thing below, toward a truth that will destroy her cover and possibly her life. She says: "Jag vet inte vad jag ska göra." *I don't know what I'm going to do.* It's the first honest thing she's said since she arrived.

### Beat 8 — Closing Image
They stand in the module doorway, the desert wind blowing dust between them. Samuel's hand is close to hers but not touching. Below their feet, twenty meters down, something ancient and alive pulses in the dark. Amma's assistentknapp records her elevated heart rate, her stress hormones, her location. Somewhere in Capitol, a screen flickers.

## Emotional Arc
Amma: Determined → awed → terrified → raw vulnerability with Samuel. Her professional armor cracks. The mission and the person beneath it begin to separate.

## Romance Beat
The first real moment between Amma and Samuel. Not attraction — recognition. Two people who have seen the same impossible thing. Honesty as intimacy. His hand near hers. Nothing more yet.

## Chapter-End Hook
Capitol is watching. Amma's biometrics just spiked in a restricted location. The clock is ticking.
