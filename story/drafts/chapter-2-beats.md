# Chapter 2 — Beat Sheet: Rytm

## Chapter Function
Amma settles into station routine, conducts first real inspections, discovers the nightly production anomaly, and has her first genuine one-on-one confrontation with Samuel — who tells her Maja found something similar in the logs, and that it took her weeks to find the right pattern.

## Chapter Goal
Establish investigative momentum. Confirm that something is wrong with modul 7-12 and the energy data. Plant Samuel as both threat and reluctant ally. End the chapter with Amma holding the first hard fact: directorate-level authorization behind an identity that doesn't appear in any personnel record.

## Tone Shift
Chapter 1 was arrival and first orientation — surfaces, impressions, controlled exposure. Chapter 2 is the first pull of the investigation: procedural inspection work, and what breaks through it. Quieter than arrival but more focused.

---

## Section 1 — Morgon (~1,200 words)

- **04:47.** Amma wakes, shoulder aching. Can't sleep; gets up before anyone else.
- Walks the grounds in pre-dawn dark. Studies modul 7-12 from ten meters: unusual corrosion on the east face — chemical, not weather-based. Photographs it. Notes: *moisture source from below, not above.*
- Breakfast — Samuel is already there. Unusual (he's not a morning person). First real exchange without the dinner table as a buffer. He asks about modul 7-12 immediately — too fast. She deflects with protocol. He accepts the deflection but the shape of it says he noted her answer.
- Viktor arrives, offers to continue the tour. He walks her through modules 3 and 5. Avoids all even-numbered modules without appearing to. She registers the pattern silently.
- 14:00 — Amma approaches modul 7-12 alone. Access panel takes three seconds to accept her code — external verification, not local. Someone is watching who enters.
- Inside: 2 degrees cooler than the regulator shows. Inverter sound is close-but-not-right. Maintenance log: Maja's last entry April 12. Next entry: May 17 — no name, only a code: *T-07-svc*. Five more entries after that, all *T-07-svc*, all routine, all unsigned.
- Maja died end of April. The service code appeared three weeks after.

**What this section does:** Establishes Amma's working method. Plants T-07-svc. Sets Viktor's evasion as a fact. Confirms Samuel is watching.

## Section 2 — Inspektion (~1,500 words)

- Formal inspections, modules 1–6. Amma moves methodically.
- **Li in module 4:** They work in silence for forty minutes. Li holds a cable casing without being asked. No words beyond "nej" and "tack." Not hostility — a deliberate kind of presence. Amma notes: Li is not absent from the world, she is selective about it.
- **Samuel in module 6:** Appears with network handset — legitimate errand, plausible route through the space. But the route is a scan. He asks about 7-12; she deflects. He mentions Maja: *"She was good at the job."* When Amma presses: *"And then she fell."* He leaves without explaining the gap between those two facts.
- **Production data review, afternoon:** Amma builds the energy output table from raw figures (not the departmental template, which rounds). Finds it: 3.7% average nightly deficit between 23:00–03:00, starting four months ago. Consistent. Scheduled. Below the automatic alert threshold of 5% — someone set that threshold, and someone is drawing precisely under it.
- Cross-reference: the deficit started four months ago. Maja died seven months in. She was here for the two months the deficit started.
- **Dinner:** Same table. Cassia looks at Amma once — two seconds, quiet — *I notice what you're doing.* Samuel scans the room twice without landing on anything. Li is absent.
- Amma's note after dinner: *Samuel. Primary function may be surveillance, not communications.*

**What this section does:** Confirms the production anomaly. Li emerges as a character. Samuel's scrutiny sharpens. Cassia watches from a distance. The investigation has a first solid fact.

## Section 3 — Natt (~1,200 words)

- **21:09** — Samuel knocks. Plausible errand: assistant button signal is weak in this corner, needs node calibration. She lets him in.
- He does the calibration. Professional. Exactly three minutes.
- Then doesn't move toward the door.
- **"Maja."** Not a question.
- He tells her: *Maja found something similar. In the logs. It took her a few weeks to find the right pattern.*
- He won't say what she found. Won't say which logs. He gives just enough to confirm the reality of what Amma is looking at — and enough to remind her that Maja searched for weeks and then died.
- He leaves without warning her off. Not advice — a data point. The shape of it: he's acknowledged she'll continue regardless, and chose to give her something useful.
- Amma processes this: he gave her information he didn't have to give. This is not neutral. Not safe alliance either. Something else.
- She runs a search on T-07-svc: no personnel record, no contractor record. Access authorization for modul 7-12: *driftsledning, direktörnivå.* Above Cassia.
- This reframes Cassia's request: *Come to me before you file.* If authorization comes from directorate level — does Cassia know? Is she trying to protect herself, or intercept?
- Amma adds the question without answering it.
- **Last image:** 23:41. Search result on screen. Mint plant on windowsill. Sixteen ceiling planks. Modul 7-12 dark against the stars — the same as every night, and nothing is the same.

**What this section does:** Samuel becomes the chapter's pivot point. T-07-svc becomes a hard fact with dangerous implications. Cassia's request gains new ambiguity. Sleep doesn't come.

## What This Chapter Does to the Story

- **Investigative thread:** Production anomaly confirmed. Directorate-level access to 7-12 under a ghost identity confirmed. The investigation now has real edges.
- **Samuel arc:** First real direct exchange with Amma, one-on-one. He chooses to share rather than report. This is his first active decision toward her rather than around her.
- **Li thread:** Opens. She is not a silent background figure. She is present and selective.
- **Viktor:** Evasion pattern established — doesn't confront, just steers. Grief for Maja running quietly.
- **Cassia thread:** Her earlier request now lands differently — knowing that something above her authorized T-07-svc raises the question of whether she knows or doesn't, and whether her request is protection or interception.
