# Chapter 1 — Arrival: Beat Sheet

## Opening Beat: The Road
- Amma in a vehicle approaching Station 7-Syd. Scorching heat. Flat, dust-colored land stretching in every direction.
- Interior: She counts. Kilometers, breaths, seconds. Coping mechanism established immediately.
- She reviews her cover mentally: Amma Wost, technician, Energy Department maintenance unit. Simple enough to remember. She's done this before.
- Shoulder aches against the seat. Sektor 14 memory flickers — she pushes it down.
- First sight of the station: low buildings, greenhouses, module towers on the horizon. Smaller than expected.

## Beat 2: Karin
- The vehicle stops. No welcoming committee. Just Karin, leaning against a doorframe, arms crossed.
- Karin's voice: short, hes, imperative. "Come in out of the sun."
- Karin shows Amma the basics: her room (small, clean, Maja's old room), the kitchen, the layout.
- The moment: Karin pauses, looks at Amma properly. "You're from the other side." Not a question.
- Amma freezes. Karin: "I wouldn't mention that to the others."
- Karin walks away. Amma stands in Maja's room. The mattress smells like someone else.

## Beat 3: The Tour — Viktor and Samuel
- Karin passes Amma off to Viktor for the station tour. Viktor is proud, loud, hard of hearing.
- Viktor's monologue: "I built half of this with these hands. When it was nothing but sand and wind and goats."
- The comic hearing exchange: Amma mentions years of experience. Viktor mishears. The bit plays.
- Viktor points out the modules in the distance. Thirteen of them. "Thirteen percent of the front's energy." He inflates the number with pride.
- Samuel appears. Lighter energy but watchful underneath. He asks questions Amma doesn't want to answer.
- Samuel's scar — Amma notices it. Neck to shoulder. Old. She wonders about the Wall.
- Samuel: "You'll like it here. Everyone does, eventually. Except the ones who don't." His smile doesn't reach his eyes.

## Beat 4: Dinner — The Ensemble
- Communal dinner. Long table, simple food. The social map reveals itself.
- Viktor holds court. Samuel fills silences. Karin eats efficiently, says nothing unnecessary.
- The twins — Elias and Nora — sit together, curious, watching Amma. "If they hoped to learn something from her, they could keep hoping."
- Li: dark features, tight bun, eats slowly and methodically. Doesn't look up. Doesn't speak. Amma notes the absence like a shape cut from the conversation.
- An empty chair. Nobody sits in it. Nobody explains.
- Tal is not present (he arrives in chapter 2).

## Beat 5: Cassia
- Cassia arrives late to dinner. The room shifts.
- Viktor sits straighter. Karin looks up. Li lifts her gaze for one moment.
- Physical description: graying curls in a loose knot, a face you can't stop watching — not beautiful, present. Light eyes that take in everything.
- Cassia sits, serves herself, apologizes for being late without making it a performance.
- She turns to Amma. Warm, direct, disarming. Treats her as an equal immediately.
- "You're Maja's replacement. Welcome." Pause. "She was good."
- Cassia asks about the journey, the department, Amma's background. Amma gives practiced answers. Cassia receives them without challenge — but her eyes say she's filing everything.

## Beat 6: The Private Word
- After dinner. Cassia catches Amma in the hallway.
- She asks Amma to walk with her. The station at twilight. Heat fading.
- Cassia talks about the farm: how it runs, what it needs. Professional.
- Then: "If you find anything unusual in the modules, I'd like you to tell me first. Before you put it in any report."
- Amma registers the request. It's wrong. A station commander shouldn't ask that.
- Cassia: "I know how it sounds. But Maja — I want to understand what happened. The department's investigation was three pages long."
- Amma agrees carefully. They part. Cassia's hand touches Amma's arm as she says goodnight. Brief. Warm.

## Closing Beat: Alone
- Amma in her room. The mattress, the aching shoulder, the dark.
- She counts ceiling tiles. A ritual. Control.
- She thinks about her mother. The Wall in the dark. Rain and panic and a baby who didn't cry.
- She thinks about Cassia's request and what it means.
- She gets up for water. In the kitchen, on the shelf — Maja's coffee cup. Unwashed but unused. Sitting exactly where she left it. No one has moved it.
- Amma stares at it. She goes back to bed.
- She doesn't sleep for a long time.
