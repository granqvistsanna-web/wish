# Chapter 1 Reviews

## Style Review — Score: 8/10

### Strengths
- Opens cleanly in medias res — counting to ten is internal discipline, not a tic. Correct Butler move.
- Sensory grounding is consistent and distributed throughout (dry heat, sand-gravel creak, mint in walls, LED under collar, shoulder burning against mattress).
- Characters introduced through behaviour, not description catalogues.
- Subtext works: the mynta exchange and "Modul 7-12" at dinner both do double duty.
- Closing sequence earns its weight. Final sentence ("Cassia hade inte sagt varför hon ville ha rapporten först. Och Amma hade inte frågat.") withholds and implicates simultaneously.

### Issues
1. **Em-dash overuse** — exceeds 2-per-page limit on multiple pages. Priority targets: dinner scene and night scene.
2. **"inte X utan Y" construction** used 8+ times — becomes a prose tic by line 199. Keep 3, cut the rest.
3. **Cassia entrance overexplains** (line ~157) — two negating clarifications after "som om rummet redan var hennes" cancel the image. Cut them.
4. **Twins absent from nighttime threat-assessment** — Amma processes Karin, Samuel, Cassia but not the twins. Add one line: "Tvillingarna: okänt."
5. **"Hon räknade fel när hon var trött"** — explains the effect instead of producing it. End on "Sex." and cut.
6. Cassia's opening line "Du är Wost" technically untagged in a multi-woman scene — add minimal attribution.

### Recommended Fixes
- Audit all em-dashes; replace with period + new sentence in most cases
- Reduce "inte X utan Y" from 8 to 3 uses
- Trust Cassia's entrance — remove the explanatory negations
- Add twins to the nighttime tally
- Cut the "räknade fel" explanation; end on "Sex."

## Character Review — Score: 9/10

### Strengths
- Amma's counting is structural, not decorative — opening, takplankor, mismeasured years.
- "Ofarliga sanning" (mynta) executed precisely and world pushes back immediately.
- Amma's danger-read of Cassia is correctly graded: it's her own reaction that's dangerous, not Cassia herself.
- Karin's voice is impeccable: imperatives, near-sentences, protection without warmth.
- Samuel's double register works: surface chattiness, "Modul 7-12" as flat statement not question.
- Viktor's hearing comedy is structurally sound; tour omission of 7-12 noted by Amma correctly.
- Cassia's entrance staged correctly — room reacts before she speaks.
- Li's silence maintained as deliberate absence; one action (lifting eyes when Cassia enters).
- Physical details accurate throughout (Amma uses left hand, Viktor's hands shaking, assistant button under collar).

### Issues
1. **Samuel's scar not established** — bible lists blekt streck från hals ner över axel as key detail and narrative question. Absent from chapter.
2. **Amma has no real-time alert during Cassia conversation** — all processing is retrospective. Should flicker in the moment.
3. **Cassia's face incompletely rendered at introduction** — "fullständigt närvarande" quality from bible not directly shown.
4. **Viktor's physical tell when Sellen is named is missing** — the hands could still briefly; shaking stopping is the unexpected concrete detail.
5. **Night-scene register slightly high** — "Att någon lade märke till att hon andades" too metaphorical for Amma at chapter 1. Should be colder, more operational.

### Recommended Fixes
- Add scar during Samuel's dinner entrance (one sentence, when his collar catches)
- Give Amma one physical/internal beat during the Cassia conversation (not only in retrospect)
- Add one clause to Cassia's introduction rendering her face as the strange thing
- Viktor: hands still when Sellen is named
- Replace "Att någon lade märke till att hon andades" with colder surveillance-language
