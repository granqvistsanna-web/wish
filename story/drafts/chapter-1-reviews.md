## Prioritized Issues

### CRITICAL

**1. Sellen's pronouns are inconsistent (hon/han)**

Sellen is male per the bible (`bible/characters/jarik-sellen.md`). But the draft uses both "hon" and "han":

- **Line 103** (Viktor): "**Hon** var tekniker i sju år" / "**Hon** kontrollerade allting tre gånger" → uses **hon** (she)
- **Line 177** (Cassia): "Sellen hette **hon**" → uses **hon** (she)
- **Line 207** (Cassia): "**Han** tyckte om det" → uses **han** (he)
- **Line 261** (narration): "dog **han** ensam" → uses **han** (he)

**Fix:** Change lines 103 and 177 to use "han" consistently, matching the bible.

---

### HIGH

**2. Em-dash overuse — 33 total, target max ~28**

Density: 2.4 per page (target: max 2). Worst stretches:

- Line 91: 3 em-dashes in one passage
- Lines 235–241: 3 em-dashes in ~100 words
- Lines 247–249: 3 em-dashes in ~100 words
- Line 259: 2 em-dashes in one sentence

**Fix:** Reduce by 8–10. Replace with colon, full stop, or rephrase. Prioritize the night scene (lines 233–270) which is densest.

**3. Sensory grounding below target**

Target: min 2 sensory details per page (~250 words). Current: ~1.4 per page (~20 details in ~3,400 words).

Gaps:
- **Dinner scene (lines 113–145):** They eat but no food is described sensorially — no taste, smell, or texture of the meal.
- **Night scene (lines 233–270):** ~500 words with only 2 sensory details (shoulder pain, humidity). No sounds from outside, no room temperature, no sheet texture.

**Fix:** Add 4–5 sensory details, focusing on dinner (what does Karin's food smell/taste like?) and night (sounds through the window, texture of the mattress, temperature shift).

**4. Adverb density slightly over limit**

Count: ~20–22 adverbs in ~3,400 words = ~1.2 per 200 words (target: <1).

Worst offenders:
- Line 21: "snabbt och effektivt" + "fientligt" (3 adverbs in one sentence)
- Line 115: "metodiskt" — could be shown instead of told
- Line 129: "noggrant och tyst" — pick one

**Fix:** Cut 3–5 adverbs. Specific suggestions:
- Line 21: drop "effektivt"
- Line 115: replace "metodiskt" with a showing gesture
- Line 129: drop either "noggrant" or "tyst"

**5. "Inte X, inte Y, utan Z" pattern repeated 3+ times**

- Line 7: "inte som Capitols sommarhetta... utan som en ugn"
- Line 21: "inte fientligt, utan som en inventering"
- Line 157: "inte som en ägarrörelse, inte som en maktgest, utan med den avspändheten..."

Also noted 5+ additional instances in previous review. Becomes a prose tic.

**Fix:** Keep 3 maximum, rephrase the rest. Line 157 (longest, most conspicuous) is the best candidate for rephrasing.

**6. Samuel's scar not visually established**

The bible specifies a prominent scar from neck to shoulder (blekt streck). Chapter 1 doesn't describe it. Should appear by chapter 2 at the latest.

**Fix:** Add one sentence during Samuel's dinner entrance (line 121–123), e.g. when his collar catches on the door handle.

**7. Viktor's physical tell when Sellen is named — missing**

Per the bible and character arc notes, Viktor's hands should briefly stop shaking when Sellen's name is spoken — one involuntary tell. This is absent from lines 103–108.

**Fix:** Add one detail: hands stilling for a moment when he says Sellen's name.

### MEDIUM

**8. Line 257 — tells rather than shows**

> "Hon hade trott att det skulle göra skillnad. Det var länge sedan. Nu visste hon bara att hon hade sett nog av kriget för att inse att hon inte ville se mer av det."

Background exposition delivered as pure reflection. Most "telling" passage in the chapter.

**Fix:** Let this emerge through a specific memory, image, or action rather than summary narration.

**9. Line 247 — generic phrasing**

> "ett litet tryck som inte var smärta och inte var något hon hade ett namn på"

"Inte var något hon hade ett namn på" is a literary cliché.

**Fix:** Replace with something more specific and physical.

**10. "Modul sju" vs "modul 7-12" naming clarity**

Line 95: Viktor shows Amma "modul tre och modul sju." If modul 7 is part of the 7-12 range, this contradicts his avoidance of that area. If modules are individually numbered and "7-12" is a separate compound unit, it's fine.

**Fix:** Clarify the naming convention — either rename "modul sju" to avoid confusion, or establish early that "7-12" is a distinct unit.

**11. "Förrförra teknikern" — Tal reference**

Line 173: Cassia says "Förrförra teknikern hade en olycka" — correctly implies the chain Sellen → Tal → Amma. The timeline confirms Tal was between them. This is **consistent**, but Tal is completely absent from the chapter otherwise.

**Fix:** Either add a one-line Tal reference elsewhere, or change to "förra teknikern" if Tal's existence shouldn't be signaled yet.

**12. Cassia's entrance overexplains (line 157)**

> "inte som en ägarrörelse, inte som en maktgest, utan med den avspändheten hos en människa som sällan behöver bevisa något"

Two negating clarifications after "som om rummet redan var hennes" dilute the image.

**Fix:** Cut the negations, trust the entrance staging.

**13. Amma has no real-time alert during Cassia conversation**

All processing of the Cassia evening conversation (lines 197–227) is retrospective in the night scene. Amma should have at least one in-the-moment physical/internal beat.

The rhetorical triplet + a fourth "något" is a bit much.

**16. Twins absent from nighttime threat-assessment**

Amma processes Karin, Samuel, and Cassia in the night scene but not the twins. Add one line: "Tvillingarna: okänt."

**17. Night-scene register slightly too metaphorical**

Too warm/metaphorical for Amma at this stage. Should be colder, more operational.

**18. Amma's career length countdown (line 251)**

> "Hon hade arbetat åtta år med att vara osynlig. Sju år. Sex. Hon räknade fel när hon var trött."

The final explanatory sentence ("Hon räknade fel när hon var trött") weakens the effect. End on "Sex." and trust the reader.

## Style Review — Detailed Findings

### What works well

- **Opening**: In medias res with immediate tension. Amma's counting established organically from line 1.
- **Dialogue**: 100% of explicit tags are "sa" — exemplary. Many lines untagged, attributed through action.
- **Character differentiation**: Each voice distinct — Karin's imperatives, Samuel's chatter, Viktor's mishearing, Li's silence, Cassia's warm authority.
- **Cassia's entrance** (lines 149–163): Room's physical reaction = Butlerian power dynamics through bodies.
- **Chapter ending**: Quiet devastation, not cheap cliffhanger. "Sömnen kom inte." — spare and earned.
- **Worldbuilding**: Through lived experience, no exposition dumps.
- **No forbidden words or phrases found.**
- **No forbidden patterns violated** (paragraph starts, full names, exclamation marks, semicolons in dialogue).
