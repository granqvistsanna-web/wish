# Forbidden Words & Phrases

These words and constructions signal AI-generated prose. Never use them.

## Forbidden Words
- delve
- tapestry
- testament
- beacon
- crucible
- paradigm
- multifaceted
- nuance / nuanced (as filler)
- landscape (metaphorical)
- realm
- fostering
- underpinning
- pivotal
- moreover
- furthermore
- utilize (use "use")
- facilitate
- leverage (as verb, unless literal)
- robust
- streamline
- whispering (of inanimate objects)
- dance/danced (of light, shadows, etc.)
- shimmering
- gossamer
- ethereal
- ineffable
- resonate / resonated
- juxtaposition
- dichotomy (unless technical)
- plethora
- myriad (as adjective)

## Forbidden Phrases
- "a testament to"
- "it's worth noting that"
- "in a world where"
- "little did they know"
- "sent shivers down"
- "couldn't help but"
- "a mix of [emotion] and [emotion]"
- "the weight of [abstraction]"
- "hung in the air"
- "pierced the silence"
- "exchange of glances"
- "unspoken understanding"
- "a sense of [emotion] washed over"
- "their eyes met across"
- "time seemed to stop"
- "electric/electricity" (for attraction, unless literal)
- "heart skipped a beat"
- "let out a breath they didn't know they were holding"

## Forbidden Patterns
- Starting 3+ paragraphs in a row with the same word
- Using a character's full name more than once per scene after introduction
- Ending more than 2 consecutive chapters on a cliffhanger
- Exclamation marks in narration (dialogue only, sparingly)
- Em-dash overuse: max 2 per page
- Semicolons in dialogue
- "As a [noun]" constructions to open sentences
