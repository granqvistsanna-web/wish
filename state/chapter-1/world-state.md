# World State — Chapter 1

## Current Conditions
- **Political situation**: Stable/status quo. Capitol controls the station remotely through assignments and communications. No active crisis beyond the ongoing module 7-12 anomaly.
- **Environmental conditions**: Desert heat, extreme sun during day, cool evenings, dust everywhere. Stars visible at night (unlike Capitol's haze).
- **Technology status**: 12 solar modules operational. Module 7-12 has a production anomaly. Communications system functional (Samuel's domain). Assistant buttons worn by most crew.
- **Social tension level**: Simmering. New arrival being assessed by everyone. Maja's death still a raw wound. Unspoken questions about what happened.

## Location Details
- **Current primary location**: Station 7-Syd — remote solar energy farm
- **Key locations established**: Perimeter gate, dining hall, sleeping quarters (Room 4 = Maja's old room), greenhouse, maintenance shed, module arrays, water tank
- **Module 7-12**: Named but not yet visited. Located among the array, site of Maja's death.

## Resources & Constraints
- **What characters have access to**: Basic housing, communal food, station infrastructure, module maintenance tools
- **What's scarce or threatened**: Information (everyone holds secrets), privacy (small isolated community), medical care (implied scarcity at frontier), trust

## Public Knowledge vs. Hidden Truth
- **What the station believes**: Maja died in an electrical accident. The production anomaly is a technical issue needing a new technician to fix. Amma is a replacement technician from Capitol.
- **What's actually true**: Amma is an undercover agent. Karin knows Amma's origins. Cassia knows more about Maja/module 7-12 than she reveals. The anomaly may be connected to something called "the thirteenth foundation." Maja may have been killed for what she found.
