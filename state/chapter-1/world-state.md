# World State — Chapter 1

## Current Conditions
- **Political situation**: Stable at macro level. The Wall stands. The Front continues. The Capitol governs. Station 7-South operates under Energy Department oversight.
- **Environmental conditions**: Arid, extreme heat during the day, cool at night. Dust pervasive. The station is isolated — nearest settlement far enough that the transport took hours.
- **Technology status**: The station's modules are operational but aging. Module 7-12 has a reported production anomaly (the official reason for Amma's assignment). Communications system functional under Samuel's management.
- **Social tension level**: Simmering. A new arrival after a death creates unease. The crew is close-knit and wary of outsiders.

## Location Details
- **Current primary location**: Station 7-South — living quarters, communal kitchen, module field visible from the station.
- **Relevant location changes**: Amma has arrived, changing the station's population from 8 to 9.

## Resources & Constraints
- **What characters have access to**: Basic food and shelter, maintenance tools, the communications system (controlled by Samuel), inspection equipment (Amma's toolkit).
- **What's scarce or threatened**: Privacy. Fresh water (implied). Contact with the outside world (communications monitored/limited). Trust.

## Public Knowledge vs. Hidden Truth
- **What the world believes**: Maja died in an electrical accident. Amma is a replacement technician from the Energy Department. The station produces energy for the Front.
- **What's actually true**: Amma is an undercover inspector. Karin knows Amma is from the wrong side of the Wall. Maja's death may not have been accidental. Module 7-12 has an anomaly that doesn't match standard malfunctions.
