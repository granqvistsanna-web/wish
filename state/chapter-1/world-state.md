# World State — Chapter 1

## Current Conditions
- **Political situation**: War on the front. Station 7-Syd supplies approximately 13% of front energy (per Viktor — figure may be exaggerated). Capitol and a wall dividing it from "the other side" are established facts. Amma is from the other side but operates under Capitol authority.
- **Environmental conditions**: Dry, extreme heat. Desert-like terrain. Sand and solar panels. No vegetation except the north greenhouse and Viktor's destillery herbs.
- **Technology status**: Assistant buttons issued to all station staff — small device monitoring health data, networking, alert-on-fall. Green LED = active. Karin does not wear one. Their true function is not yet fully explained.
- **Social tension level**: Simmering — Sellen's death unresolved, replacement inspector (Amma) has just arrived, Cassia has made a private request that complicates official channels.

- **What's scarce or threatened**: Truth about Sellen's death. Trust between personnel and the incoming inspector. Whatever was in modul 7-12 that Sellen found.

## Public Knowledge vs. Hidden Truth
- **What the world believes**: Sellen's death = electrical accident at modul 7-12. Routine. The new inspector is here for standard maintenance review.
