# Character State — Chapter 1

## Amma

### Emotional State
- **Current mood/mindset**: Hyper-vigilant, controlled. Performing the role of competent technician. Counting everything — steps, seconds, ceiling tiles.
- **Unresolved tension**: Karin identified her as being from the wrong side of the Wall. Her cover may already be compromised.
- **What they want right now**: Get through the first day without exposure. Establish herself as unremarkable.

### Relationship Status
- **With Karin**: Wary respect. Karin knows something about her origins but chose to warn rather than expose. Trust level: low but significant.
- **With Viktor**: Surface-level. He's curious about her credentials but easily managed. She lets him talk.
- **Recent shift**: Karin's warning changed the dynamic from "new arrival being assessed" to "someone with a secret being protected — conditionally."

### Knowledge
- **What she now knows**: The station layout, the crew size, that the previous technician (Maja) died at Module 7-12, that her cover has a visible crack (her body doesn't match her documentation).
- **What she still doesn't know**: What Maja was doing at the module. Who else on the station is suspicious of her. What the assistant buttons do.
- **What she suspects**: Maja's death wasn't an accident. Karin knows more than she's saying.

### Physical State
- **Location at chapter end**: Her assigned quarters, lying awake.
- **Physical condition**: Shoulder aching from the transport. Dehydrated from the heat. Otherwise functional.
- **Notable possessions**: Inspection toolkit, falsified credentials (Amma Wost, Energy Department), personal datapad.
