# Character State — Chapter 2

## Amma

### Emotional State
- **Current mood/mindset**: Unsettled. Cassia disarmed her. Tal unnerved her. The performance of being Amma Wost is already harder than expected.
- **Unresolved tension**: Cassia asked her to report to Cassia first, not Capitol. This complicates the chain of command and Amma's mission. Tal followed her outside — is she being surveilled?
- **What they want right now**: Begin the actual inspection tomorrow. Get distance from the social dynamics. Do her job and leave.

### Relationship Status
- **With Cassia**: Drawn in despite herself. Cassia's warmth feels genuine but calculated. Trust level: conflicted.
- **With Samuel**: Wary. He talks too much but watches too closely. He's the one who could catch her on the communications system.
- **With Karin**: Stable wariness. Karin protects her secret but doesn't explain why.
- **With Tal**: Hostile. He followed her outside. She doesn't know his agenda.
- **With Viktor**: Comfortable distance. He's harmless and amusing.
- **With Li**: Noted her silence. Curious but not threatened.
- **Recent shift**: Cassia's request to be the first point of contact changed Amma's understanding of the station's power structure. It's not just the Capitol watching.

### Knowledge
- **What she now knows**: The full crew roster and dynamics. Cassia runs the station with genuine authority. Tal replaced Maja recently. Li is an enigma — no one explains her role. Samuel manages the comms system and is observant. The assistant buttons are standard issue but not universally worn.
- **What she still doesn't know**: What Cassia really wants from her. Why Tal followed her. What Li does. What Maja found at Module 7-12.
- **What she suspects**: Cassia knows more about Maja's death than she admits. Tal's hostility is personal, not just territorial.

### Physical State
- **Location at chapter end**: Her quarters, awake. Someone is walking the perimeter outside — footsteps stopping near her window.
- **Physical condition**: Shoulder ache persistent. Adjusting to the heat. Tired from performing.
- **Notable possessions**: Same as Chapter 1.
